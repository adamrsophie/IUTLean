Open problems

Every unresolved obligation of the project, grouped by kind. Each item gives an id, a precise statement, the source page (manuscript pages, see docs/SOURCE_MANIFEST.md), a category, and what would close it. The per-statement records in data/curated/ carry the same obligations in their obligations fields; counts are in docs/DASHBOARD.md. Published claims bearing on these problems, with their stated status, are tabulated in docs/LITERATURE.md, Section 3.

The status BLOCKED_BY_MISSING_JUSTIFICATION used in data/curated/ means: the text gives an assertion where a formalization needs a derivation; this is not a claim that the result is false.

The seven categories are kept apart:

  1. failure of proof search;
  2. missing library theorem;
  3. gap in the formalization;
  4. insufficient hypotheses;
  5. counterexample to a simplified abstraction;
  6. counterexample to a faithfully formalized statement;
  7. demonstrated contradiction.

No item below is in category 6 or 7. The countermodels of IUT/Countermodels/ are in category 5: each refutes a simplified model of a step, not a statement of the papers (docs/COUNTERMODELS.md). Category 4 means that the hypotheses written on the page do not by themselves yield the step in a formalization. It does not mean that the step is false, and an unstated hypothesis that holds is not an error in the paper.

0. Next stage: typed objects of the final comparison

Started: T-CMP has an abstract interface, IUT.PaperIII.Corollary312.Comparison. Its objects are inputs (an abstract region log-volume, required only to be monotone, a hull map with (P1) to (P3), a q-pilot region, a family of possible images), not the papers' objects. The logarithm of a measure is one instance of such a log-volume, but not the general case: the weighted packet log-volume of pages 95 to 97 and 115 to 117 is in general not the logarithm of a measure on the original product, and the weights of Rem 3.1.1 (ii), page 94, already divide by the local degree. The interface omits the construction of every region from initial Θ-data, the generation of the possible images by (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3) (they are an arbitrary indexed family), the tensor packets and the procession structure (only an equal-weight average over labels), the sum over places, the normalization of the integral structure (it enters only as the sign of the q-pilot log-volume), and the identification of the two sides through the link (both regions are placed in one space with one log-volume by the typing). It states the comparison directly against the hull of the union of the possible images, as a log-volume inequality (LogVolumeComparison) and as a region containment (Containment). Step (xi-f), page 184, asserts a numerical inclusion, which is the inequality form; containment of regions is a sufficient condition studied in the interface, not what the page asserts. It proves that containment together with a finiteness hypothesis gives the numerical conclusion (conclusion_of_containment), and gives examples on ℝ where the comparison holds, fails, and needs the hull of the union. T-PKT, T-IMG and T-HULL are not started. T-ABC is started at the level of statements (IUT/Applications/ABCSpecialization.lean); the gap between it and IUT IV is T-GENELL.

1. Toward IUT III, Corollary 3.12

Corollary 3.12 (IUT III, pages 173 to 174) asserts −|log(Θ)| ∈ ℝ and −|log(Θ)| ≥ −|log(q)|. The analysis, with the notation μ̄, Q, Ū, G, τ, ⊑ used below, is in docs/COR312_ANALYSIS.md. Its numerical layer is in IUT/PaperIII/Corollary312/Numerical.lean.
