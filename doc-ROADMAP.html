Roadmap

A staged plan, with IUT III Corollary 3.12 first. Each stage has entry and exit criteria that can be checked in the repository. A stage may end with a precise obstruction instead of a proof; that counts as an exit when the obstruction is stated in Lean or recorded in OPEN_PROBLEMS.md with its category.

Stage 0: infrastructure (done)

  • Exit: the trust checks of docs/VERIFICATION_REPORT.md pass in CI; Challenge/Defs.lean is fingerprinted; the catalog pipeline regenerates docs/DASHBOARD.md with no record claiming a verified status that fails the checks.

Stage 1: the numerical layer and IUT IV (present stage, done in part)

  • Entry: stage 0.
  • Work: the arithmetic of IUT IV §1 and §2 conditional on Corollary 3.12, and the numerical layer of Corollary 3.12 itself.
  • Exit: every step of Theorem 1.10 and Corollaries 2.2 and 2.3 has a Lean declaration named by its record in data/curated/iut4.json, with status KERNEL_VERIFIED or CONDITIONALLY_VERIFIED and the hypothesis listed; the passage from Theorem A to Challenge.ABCLog is either proved or recorded with the missing definitions (heights, log-differents, log-conductors).

Stage 1a: typed objects of the final comparison (next)

The present library states numbers, not objects: PilotLogVolumes takes −|log(Θ)| and −|log(q)| as given. The next stage states the comparison of Corollary 3.12 on typed objects for one place, and the specialization of IUT IV to abc triples, before the full vocabulary of stage 2 exists. Each step is small and has its own acceptance criterion.

  • Entry: stage 1 as it stands; the fragment rule of docs/DECISIONS.md, decision 12, applied to the curated records.
  • Literature survey of 2026-11-25 (LITERATURE.md): elementary Lean targets and comparison points for Challenge.ABC from the survey feed stage 1a.
  • Work, in order:
    1. Local tensor packet with its normalization. For one v_ℚ and a label set A, a type for the tensor packet log(^A F_{v_ℚ}) (IUT III pages 92 to 99), its integral structures and the maps between them as described on page 93, and the packet-normalized log-volume with the normalization of Proposition 3.9 (i) (page 115): the integral structure has log-volume 0 and multiplication by p_v adds −log(p_v). Acceptance: a Lean structure whose fields are data, not conclusions; a proof of the two normalization rules from the definition; an example with A a singleton. Partly done on finite levels: weights and the packet-normalization on direct product regions (WeightedPacket.lean); the inductive limits of page 93 are not. Normalization part met: IUT/Prerequisites/PadicVolume.lean (padicMuLog_unitBall, padicMuLog_p_smul_unitBall) for one finite extension and IUT/PaperIV/LocalFields/Packets.lean (packet_muLog_normalization_tensor) for a finite packet over ℚ_p. Still open: the integral structures and maps of page 93. The packet over ℤ_p is identified with its image over ℚ_p for any finite number of factors (IUT/PaperIV/LocalFields/PacketIdentifications.lean).
    2. Admissible images under the indeterminacies. A type for the region space, and the set of admissible images of a region under the group generated by (Ind1) and (Ind2) together with the (Ind3) containments (IUT III pages 154 to 156), as a set defined by a formula. Acceptance: the set is defined, not assumed; invariance of the normalized log-volume under the generated group (candidate condition C2) is stated as a Prop on these objects and either proved or recorded as an obligation with its category.
    3. The hull. The holomorphic hull of the union of admissible images, extending IUT/PaperIII/Remark3_9_5/HullOperator.lean from the abstract operator to the packet. Acceptance: a Lean definition with the intersection formula proved on the packet.
    4. The comparison on those objects. Started as an interface: IUT/PaperIII/Corollary312/Comparison.lean states the comparison as a region containment and as a log-volume inequality on abstract inputs (a measure, a hull map with (P1) to (P3), a q-pilot region, an arbitrary family of possible images), proves the passage to the numerical layer, and gives examples where it holds, fails, and needs the union. It omits the objects of steps 1 to 3: regions are inputs, the possible images are not generated by the indeterminacies, there are no tensor packets, no procession structure beyond an equal-weight average, no sum over places, and no identification of the two sides through the link. The step is met only when the interface is instantiated by steps 1 to 3. The statement −|log(q)| ≤ μ̄(hull of the union of admissible images of the Θ-pilot region) as a Prop whose terms are the objects of steps 1 to 3, with finiteness of the hull side (−|log(Θ)| ∈ ℝ) as a separate conjunct; a proof that it implies the numerical statement of IUT/PaperIII/Corollary312/Numerical.lean. Acceptance: the Prop contains no field or hypothesis that restates it; the implication to the numerical layer is kernel checked; the comparison itself remains an obligation unless proved.
    5. IUT IV Theorem A at abc triples. The formal specialization of IUT IV Theorem A to an abc triple (a, b, c): the point x = a/c of the projective line minus the three points 0, 1, ∞, with its height, log-different and log-conductor computed in terms of a, b, c, so that Theorem A, taken as a named hypothesis, implies Challenge.ABCLog. Acceptance: the height and conductor computations are proved for every coprime triple; the implication is kernel checked with Theorem A as the only unproved hypothesis, stated with the heights and divisors of the paper, not with arbitrary real functions.
  • Exit: steps 1 to 5 each met their acceptance criterion or are recorded in OPEN_PROBLEMS.md with a category and the smallest failing check.

Stage 2: the vocabulary of Corollary 3.12

  • Entry: stage 1a; the ambiguities A1 (averaging index) and A2 (normalization) of OPEN_PROBLEMS.md have a recorded reading, with the reason.
  • Work, in order: the p-adic logarithm properties needed for log-shells (P-LOG); log-shells and their mono-analytic versions; tensor packets and their procession-normalized log-volumes (IUT III Proposition 3.9); the holomorphic hull on packets (extending IUT/PaperIII/Remark3_9_5/HullOperator.lean).
  • Exit: Lean definitions of these objects with no field that assumes a conclusion of the papers, each module citing its pages; Proposition 3.9 (i) and (iii) stated and proved or recorded as blocked with a category.

Stage 3: the indeterminacies and the two log-volumes

  • Entry: stage 2.
  • Work: definitions of (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3) (obligations OB-IND, A3 to A6); a definition of the set of possible images; definitions of −|log(Θ)| and −|log(q)| (OB-DEF).
  • Exit: PilotLogVolumes of Numerical.lean is instantiated from these definitions, not given as data; condition C4 (common normalization) proved or refuted.

Stage 4: from Theorem 3.11 to Corollary 3.12

  • Entry: stage 3.
  • Work: a faithful statement of Theorem 3.11 (G-311, OB-311-HYP); then C1, C2, C3, C7, and the condition of Step (xi-f) in one of its readings C5, C5' or C5 with C8.
  • Exit: one of three outcomes, each checkable in Lean:
    1. Corollary 3.12 proved from Theorem 3.11 and the named conditions, all proved;
    2. Corollary 3.12 proved conditionally, with each unproved condition a named hypothesis that is not a restatement of the conclusion (C6 is excluded);
    3. a countermodel to a faithfully formalized intermediate statement, with the statement traced to its pages.

Stage 5: IUT I and II as needed by stage 4

  • Entry: stage 4 has identified which constructions of IUT I and II its definitions need.
  • Work: prime strips, Hodge theaters and Θ-links, only as far as stage 4 requires, starting with poly-morphisms and capsules (IUT I §0). Started: IUT/PaperI/PolyMorphism.lean defines poly-morphisms, poly-isomorphisms, the full poly-isomorphism, their composition, capsules and capsule morphisms (IUT I pages 33 and 34); the category laws for capsules are not proved, and the capsule-full poly-isomorphism is stated only through the bijectivity of the index map.
  • Exit: the statements of IUT I and II on the critical path of docs/CRITICAL_PATH.md that stage 4 uses have status STATEMENT_FORMALIZED or a blocking status with a reason.

Stage 6: review

  • Entry: any stage exit.
  • Work: a reading of every new Lean statement against the rendered pages by someone other than its author; a recheck of OPEN_PROBLEMS.md categories.
  • Exit: the statement status of reviewed records is STATEMENT_REVIEWED.

Progress against these stages is measured in docs/DASHBOARD.md.
