Critical path to abc

Generated by scripts/build_graph.py. Do not edit by hand.

Method

Start from IUT4.ThmA and IUT4.Cor2_3 (the diophantine inequalities of IUT IV from which the papers derive abc) and follow, transitively, every citation in a statement and in a proof, together with the edges that curated records add (parts, proof steps, results used, external dependencies on the other papers). Every statement reached is on the critical path. Fragments are not followed. Citations in the paragraphs around a lettered result are not followed; following them as well would add IUT3.ThmA, IUT3.ThmB.

Citation edges are mechanical. A citation of B in the text of A is recorded as an edge A to B whether or not A logically depends on B: the text cites results for comparison, motivation and notation as well as for proof steps. The graph therefore over-approximates logical dependence in that respect. It also under-approximates it: citations not written as "Kind N.M" (by section, by display, by unnumbered discussion), citations whose target is not indexed, and uses that the text does not cite at all are not edges. The critical path is therefore neither an upper nor a lower bound on what the derivation of abc uses; it is a reading list for checking that derivation.

There is no Lean edge from Challenge.ABC to IUT IV Theorem A (IUT4.ThmA) or to Corollary 2.3: the reduction of the abc conjecture to these statements is not formalized, and neither statement is stated in Lean. The roots of the critical path are therefore statements of the papers, not the Lean target.

Missing roots: none.
