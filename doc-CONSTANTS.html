"Kernel checked" means the arithmetic in which the constant appears is proved in Lean; "conditional" means it is proved relative to named hypotheses that encode unformalized inputs. Unless stated, Lean names are in the namespace IUT.PaperIV.Theorem1_10 (modules under IUT/PaperIV/Theorem1_10/).
