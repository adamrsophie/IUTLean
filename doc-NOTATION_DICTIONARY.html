Notation dictionary

This file merges the four per-paper dictionaries by topic. Each symbol is listed with the paper and the page where it is defined or first fixed. Page numbers are those of the author manuscripts listed in docs/SOURCE_MANIFEST.md (IUT I May 2020, IUT II December 2020, IUT III May 2020, IUT IV April 2020), not the journal pages.

The per-paper files remain the reference: they record which entries were checked against a rendered page and which were read from the extracted text only.

The Tate parameter, its root, the q-pilot, and the indices

The papers use one letter for several related objects; a formalization must keep them apart. Checked against the rendered IUT I page 71, IUT II page 72 and IUT IV page 23; the Scholze and Stix manuscript, page 3, uses the same normalization.

ObjectDefinitionRelationWhere
Tate parameter q_v (single underline under v)the q-parameter of E_v over K_v, an element of O^▷q_v = qq_v^{2l}IUT I 71
chosen root qq_v (q_v with a double underline)q_v^{1/2l}; exists by Definition 3.1 (b), (c); determined only up to a μ_{2l}-multiple; it is the value of Θ_v (double underline; the reciprocal of an l-th root of the theta function, IUT I 70) at √(−q_v)qq_v^{2l} = q_vIUT I 71
theta value at label jthe μ_{2l}-orbit of qq_v^{j²}, for j ∈ {0, 1, …, l*} determined by j ∈ |F_l|qq_v^{j²} = q_v^{j²/2l} (Tate parameter q_v)IUT II 72
q-pilotbuilt from qq_v, not from the Tate parameter q_vits absolute log-volume is |log(q)| = (1/2l)·log(𝔮)IUT IV 23
log(𝔮)normalized degree of the effective arithmetic divisor of the Tate parameters at the bad placesno 1/2l factorIUT IV 23
deg(q_E), deg(q̲_E)Scholze and Stix: the arithmetic divisor of the Tate parameters, and of the chosen rootsdeg(q̲_E) = deg(q_E)/(2ℓ)SS 2 to 4

So a quantity written with q must say whether it is the Tate parameter or the chosen root: the two differ by the exponent 2l (by 1/2l on log-volumes), and the theta values carry the exponent j² on the root, not on the Tate parameter. The symbol qq_v for the double-underlined q is the one used in docs/notation/IUT2.md.

Packet and procession indices. The labels are |F_l| = {0} ⊔ F*_l, identified with {0, 1, …, l*} (IUT II 3; IUT III 153 to 154). The procession at label j uses the capsule index set S^±_{j+1} = {0, …, j} of j + 1 elements (IUT III 120, 153; IUT IV 27), and the tensor packet over a label set A is log(^A F_{v_ℚ}) (IUT III 92 to 99). The averaging index of the procession-normalized log-volume is written F*_l on IUT III pages 173 and 181 and |F_l| on pages 153, 174 and 183 (ambiguity A1 of OPEN_PROBLEMS.md); the two differ by the label 0. The theta exponent j² uses the representative j ∈ {0, …, l*}.
