What is trusted

  • The Lean kernel of Lean 4.36.0.
  • Mathlib at tag v4.36.0, commit fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842: its definitions and the soundness of what it has proved.
  • The three axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.
  • **Challenge/Defs.lean**, reviewed by hand: rad, ABC, ABCFinite, ABCLog. A reader must check that these say what the abc conjecture says.
  • **The seven statements of Challenge.lean** (abc_iff_abcFinite, abc_iff_abcLog, rad_eq_radical, coprime_iff_pairwise, theorem1_10_final_arithmetic, scholzeStix_with_squares, scholzeStix_without_squares). lake comparator checks that Solution proves exactly these statements; it cannot check that the statements are the intended ones. The fingerprint check (scripts/Controls/fingerprints.py) records the elaborated types of these statements and the list of names in comparator.json, so a change to them is detected; but a change made together with a new record (--write) passes, so the record is only as good as the separate approval of its changes. Each of the seven statements, and any change to it, needs a human mathematical comparison with its source, approved separately from the change itself.
  • The transcription from the papers. Every Lean statement that formalizes a statement of the papers depends on a human reading of the PDF. Source review versus kernel acceptance

Two different things are established, by different means, and neither implies the other.

  • Kernel acceptance (lake build, the axiom gate, lake comparator, lake check): each Lean declaration is proved as written from the allowed axioms. This is mechanical.
  • Source review: a Lean statement says what the cited statement of the papers says, with the same objects, hypotheses, quantifiers and conclusion. This is a human comparison of the Lean type with the rendered page. It is needed for every correspondence between a statement of the papers and a Lean declaration (every lean_declaration of data/curated/, every docstring that names a statement of the papers, the seven statements of Challenge.lean), not only for Challenge/Defs.lean. No script performs it. A record that links a Lean lemma checking only one step of a statement is a fragment record (kind: "fragment") and gives the parent statement no formalized or verified status.

Pins and exclusions

  • Toolchain leanprover/lean4:v4.36.0 (lean-toolchain); Mathlib tag v4.36.0, commit fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842 (lakefile.toml, lake-manifest.json).
  • Gates configured in CI, in order: the source scan, the root check, lake build, lake build Challenge, the fingerprint check, the axiom gate, the gate controls, lake comparator and lake check (.github/workflows/axiom-gate.yml). This report describes what each establishes; it does not record the outcome of a particular run. The outcome for a given revision is the CI log of that revision.
  • Excluded from the gate by design: IUT/Experimental/ (drafts, never imported) and Challenge.lean (statements with sorry, not a default target, checked only through lake comparator). The axiom gate detects statements of the abc conjecture only up to definitional equality. The source scan is syntactic. lake comparator and lake check run only on Linux.

Registries of open statements

Check 3 of AxiomGate.lean enforces the rule that no declaration of the library proves an open statement without assuming one. The open statements are three plain lists near the top of AxiomGate.lean; an entry is the full name of a definition and the module that declares it, and adding one needs no change of the logic.

  • closedForms: open statements of type Prop related to the abc conjecture. First those that the library proves equivalent to it: Challenge.ABC, Challenge.ABCFinite, Challenge.ABCLog, the three forms of IUT.ABC.FormalConjectures, IUT.ABC.Variants.ABCInt, IUT.ABC.Variants.ABCIntSum, and IUT.ABCSpecialization.VojtaLineMinusThreePointsRational and ...UnitInterval; then the open statement IUT.VojtaNumberField.VojtaLineMinusThreePointsBoundedDegree, which implies it; and, in IUT.CompactlyBounded, the open statements iutIVCor2_3ReducedFormStatement, abcRatioBoundedStatement, cbDegreeOneAllStatement and cbDegreeOneSupportFreeStatement, which abc or the bounded degree statement implies and which are not proved equivalent to it. Neither a form nor its negation may be proved outright.
  • openFamilies: constants of type … → Prop some of whose instances are open: IUT.ABC.Variants.ABCWithExponent (open for κ > 1) and IUT.ABCSpecialization.RationalPointBoundFor and IUT.VojtaNumberField.VojtaLineMinusThreePointsDegreeAtMost (open for d ≥ 1, trivially true for d = 0), IUT.CompactlyBounded.cbDegreeAtMostStatement (trivially true for d = 0), IUT.CompactlyBounded.genEllTheorem2_1iiStatement (trivial when the set of primes contains a non-prime), IUT.ABC.Unconditional.uncondABCRestrictedToStatement (equivalent to Challenge.ABC for D ≥ 1, vacuously true for D = 0), and IUT.HeightExtension.htExtDegreeAtMostAnyFieldStatement (proved equivalent to VojtaLineMinusThreePointsDegreeAtMost with the same constants, vacuously true for d = 0). No instance may be proved outright, for any argument; negated instances may (not_abcWithExponent_of_le_one refutes κ ≤ 1), and so may implications whose hypothesis is an instance (abcWithExponent_mono).
  • authorStatements: statements of results or claims of other papers that the library may assume but never proves: Thm5_4Claim, Thm5_3iiRationalClaim, Lemma5_7Claim and Cor5_8Statement of IUT.ExplicitEstimates, and ZhouTheorem32i, ZhouCorollary33, ZhouNLevelBound and ZhouComputedRange of IUT.Applications.ZhouExplicit, and SouleTheoremBClaim, LaniewskiMullerMassBound, PastenDivisorBound and DivisorCountPowerBound of IUT.ABC.Elementary. The module IUT/Applications/DupuyHilado.lean declares no such statement: its conditional lemmas take inequalities between real numbers as hypotheses.

A further list, provedInstances, accepts a declaration that concludes a registered statement without assuming one, by full name and the hash of its statement (the gate prints the hash in its report); it is empty. A proved instance that is not open, such as a future theorem VojtaLineMinusThreePointsDegreeAtMost 0, goes there.

To register a statement, add one line (Full.Name, Module.Name), to closedForms, openFamilies or authorStatements in the section "Registries of check 3" of AxiomGate.lean; nothing else changes.

How a statement is compared: the whole statement, and then each conclusion after stripping the leading ∀ binders one at a time (after reducible unfolding) until the statement is no longer a ∀, each with its conjuncts and with each side of an ↔ whose other side mentions no registered constant, split to any depth, is compared with c ?x₁ … ?xₙ for each entry c and fresh metavariables, and with its negation for closed forms and author statements. Both sides are first expanded: every definition of the project (IUT.*, Challenge.*, Solution, the file being checked; instances excepted) and Not are unfolded, whatever their reducibility. Then isDefEq runs at the transparency of instances, so definitions of Mathlib stay folded and a near miss (an exponent 3 : ℕ against κ : ℝ, an extra + 0 on ℝ) fails at once instead of unfolding the reals; the control ExponentThree checks this. A local def Y : Prop := Challenge.ABC followed by theorem t : Y is caught, since Y is expanded, and so is an alias marked @[irreducible]. Not caught: a statement equal to a registered one only after unfolding a Mathlib definition, and an alias that hides the ∀, ∧ or ↔ around a registered statement (def Z : Prop := 1 = 1 ∧ ABC; the binders and connectives are seen up to reducible unfolding only). Each statement has a heartbeat budget; running out of it is an error that names the statement, never a pass. The binder walk and the split of ∧ and ↔ have no small bound of their own: their fuel equals the budget counted in heartbeats, and running out of it is reported as an incomplete inspection, also an error. A declaration named abcConjecture is announced as a resolution only when its whole statement, with no hypothesis, is a registered closed form or its negation; with any other statement it is an error, so the name alone never announces a resolution. A match is accepted when a stripped hypothesis mentions a registered constant by name. The limits: propositional reformulations that are not definitionally equal to a registered statement, and a hypothesis that mentions a registered constant without assuming it are not detected; an alias of a registered statement used as a hypothesis is not recognized as an assumption, so such a declaration is reported (this fails closed). An entry whose module the import closure does not contain is skipped with a note, and is an error under continuous integration.

Known limits

Errors of the extracted text found against renders. Each was corrected in the Lean statement or the record; none is an error in the papers.

  • IUT III page 174: the text reads V^bad (= ∅); the render reads (≠ ∅).
  • IUT II page 158: the text drops the negation in V^bad (≠ ∅) in Definition 4.9 (viii).
  • IUT IV page 10: the ceiling in a_i and the floor in b_i of Proposition 1.2 are dropped.
  • IUT IV pages 13 to 14: the floor in Proposition 1.4 (iii) is dropped, which accounts for a +1.
  • IUT IV page 22: l = 5 in the text; l ≠ 5 in the render.
  • IUT IV page 25: ∉ read as ∈ in Step (iii) of Theorem 1.10.
  • IUT IV page 40: p ∈ A in the text of Proposition 2.1; p ∉ A in the render.
  • IUT IV page 46: V^bad_mod = ∅ in the text; ≠ ∅ in the render.
  • IUT I: the arrow decorations of Definition 1.1, the bars of Theorem 2.6 and the ⋊ of Definition 6.1 are lost in the text; the last was restored from renders.
  • Report of Mochizuki, page 18: a ≠ is dropped.

Pages not read, or read only at the level of statements.

  • IUT III Remarks 1.5.1 to 1.5.3 (pages 52 to 55) were not read; Remark 1.5.4 cites Remark 1.5.2 (ii).
  • IUT III Proposition 2.1 and Corollary 2.3 (pages 58 onward) are cited by Remark 3.8.2 but were not read.
  • IUT I Propositions 2.1, 2.2, 2.4 and Corollaries 2.3, 2.5 were read at the level of statements only; their proofs were not checked. IUT I Example 5.1 (pages 123 to 131) was read only in part.
  • IUT IV Proposition 1.8 (iv), on which IUT I Remark 3.1.5 depends, was not read.
  • The corrections to [EtTh] recorded in IUT I Remarks 3.1.6 and 3.2.4 were not checked against [EtTh]. Parts of outside references were read for the modules that cite them, among them [GenEll] pages 3 to 13 and its corrections, [EtTh] Section 1 (pages 18 to 21), The Geometry of Frobenioids I pages 10 to 25 and 27, and the author's comments on [EtTh], on The Geometry of Frobenioids I and on [SemiAnbd]; each module docstring lists the pages read and those checked against rendered images.
  • The Scholze and Stix manuscript quotes an earlier version of Step (xi) than the May 2020 manuscript; see docs/SCHOLZE_STIX_ANALYSIS.md.
  • Statements with status NOT_EXTRACTED in docs/DASHBOARD.md were located by the mechanical index only.

Name resolution. The gate compares names, not the elaborated meaning of identifiers in source files. A reader must still check by eye: that an identifier in a statement means the constant its spelling suggests when a project constant of the same name exists in an enclosing project namespace (project names shadowing project names are not reported); members of a project type used inside namespace S of that type; the effect of open of a library namespace; and the three allowlisted names of check 6 (shadowAllowed in AxiomGate.lean).

Checks that run only in CI. lake comparator and lake check need Linux. A contributor on another system relies on CI for them.

The catalog. Citation edges are mechanical and over-approximate dependence (docs/DECISIONS.md, decision 8). The scripts are untrusted; their output can be recomputed from the inputs.
