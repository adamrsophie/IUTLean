Source
Neutral status. The abc conjecture is not proved here. The project neither assumes that inter-universal Teichmüller theory is correct nor that it is wrong; a countermodel to a simplified abstraction is not a counterexample to the papers.

Milestones

Each entry is a verified commit on the main branch, oldest first, generated from the git history. Times are local (UTC−5); the date is shown when it changes.

Classes: statement cataloged fragment checked in Lean full statement proved in Lean trust tooling documentation. A class says what kind of change a commit is; only the class full statement proved in Lean says that a statement record of the papers is proved, and only for the records that data/dashboard.json counts as proved in full. Housekeeping commits are drawn as small entries.

No milestone so far proves the abc conjecture or formalizes IUT III, Corollary 3.12. The project takes no side in the dispute about the theory.

    Eleven modules: towers, tensor packets, divisor pull-back and compactly bounded subsets. a274e72 fragment checked in Lean

    IUT IV, Section 1 gains Proposition 1.3 over a base field between Q_p and k_i: part (i) in full, and part (ii) in the tame case, when p does not divide the degree, or under a bound on the exponent of the different. Fragments that check a setting chosen here, an inference or an example are counted apart as model tests. The abc conjecture and Corollary 3.12 are not proved here.
