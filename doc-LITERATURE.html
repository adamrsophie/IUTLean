Literature survey

This page summarizes a survey of the arXiv literature around inter-universal Teichmüller theory (IUT), the abc conjecture, and the formalization methods this project uses. The machine-readable record is data/literature.json, with one entry per paper read and per paper kept at screening. Each entry gives authors, title, year, arXiv categories, a theme, a relevance score, how the paper was read, a neutral description, the statements of interest with their numbers (for the papers scored 2 or 3), and links to statements, open problems and files of this project.

The project is neutral in the dispute over IUT III, Corollary 3.12. This page reports what papers state and what readings for this project found. It does not say whether any paper proves or refutes abc, Szpiro's conjecture, Vojta's conjecture or Corollary 3.12.

1. Method and counts

  • Search. 96 queries to the arXiv API, retrieved on 2026-11-25, returned 1,412 distinct records (title, authors, abstract, categories, dates).
  • Screening. Each record was screened on its title and abstract: 201 were included, 12 were marked maybe, and 1,199 were excluded.
  • Reading. 98 papers (86 of the included ones and all 12 maybes) were selected for reading in full text. Of these, 32 were read in full from the TeX source, 30 in full from the PDF text, 35 in part (the sections read are listed in each record), and 1 could not be read because the arXiv page states that it is withdrawn. The other 115 included papers were not read beyond the abstract. One further working paper, not on arXiv, was supplied to the project and read in part.
  • Records. data/literature.json therefore holds 214 records: 98 read from arXiv, 115 abstract only, and the supplied working paper.
  • Scores. Each paper read was given a relevance score from 0 (no use for the project) to 3 (directly usable): 7 papers scored 3, 24 scored 2, 43 scored 1 and 25 scored 0. Papers known only from the abstract have no score.
  • Second readings. For the papers that bear most on the comparison with Corollary 3.12, a second, independent reading checked selected statements. An internal inconsistency of a paper is reported on this page only when both readings report the same thing at the same location.

Where a paper's TeX title uses a double hyphen, it is written with a single hyphen in the records; a spaced hyphen used as a separator in a title is written as a colon; TeX markup in titles is reduced to plain text.

IUT core. T. Dupuy and A. Hilado, "The Statement of Mochizuki's Corollary 3.12: Initial Theta Data" (arXiv:2004.13228), restate the hypotheses of Corollary 3.12 (pre theta data, Definition 2.4.1; initial theta data, Definition 2.8.1) in standard terms and work the example of curve 11a1 with l = 13. Their Remark 2.4.2 records that IUT I, Definition 3.1 asks for 6-torsion while the authors impose 30-torsion because IUT IV uses it. A. Sarkisyan (arXiv:2306.02448) claims another proof of Corollary 3.12; see Section 3.

Joshi series. K. Joshi's papers build "arithmetic Teichmüller spaces" from untilts of perfectoid fields and Fargues and Fontaine curves (arXiv:2010.05748, 2106.11452, 2210.11635), anabelomorphy (arXiv:2003.01890, 2008.01228) and arithmeticoids (arXiv:2305.10398). Three papers carry the main claims: a local prototype (arXiv:2303.01662), a version of Corollary 3.12 for theta sets the author constructs (arXiv:2401.13508), and abc (arXiv:2403.10430). A report (arXiv:2505.10568) states the author's position without proofs. Section 1.4 of arXiv:2403.10430 gives the author's correspondence with IUT IV: Corollary 2.2 with Theorem 5.7.1, Corollary 3.12 with Corollary 9.11.1.1 of arXiv:2401.13508, Theorem 1.10 with Theorem 6.1.1, and Corollary 2.3 with Theorem 7.1.1.

Explicit consequences. These papers derive explicit inequalities from Corollary 3.12 or from IUT IV, and state them as conditional:

  • T. Dupuy and A. Hilado, "Probabilistic Szpiro, Baby Szpiro, and Explicit Szpiro from Mochizuki's Corollary 3.12" (arXiv:2004.13108);
  • V. Dimitrov, "Effectivity in Mochizuki's work on the abc-conjecture" (arXiv:1601.03572), an outline of an effective form of [GenEll], Theorem 2.1;
  • Z.-P. Zhou, "The inter-universal Teichmüller theory and new Diophantine results over the rational numbers. I" and "II" (arXiv:2503.14510, 2510.05448), which take as input the μ₆ version of Corollary 3.12 from S. Mochizuki, I. Fesenko, Y. Hoshi, A. Minamide and W. Porowski, "Explicit Estimates in Inter-universal Teichmüller Theory" (not returned by the search, so outside this survey);
  • P. Mihăilescu and M. T. Rassias (arXiv:2108.08580), a Fermat bound announced as input for an explicit abc inequality based on IUT IV, Theorem A.

Critiques and the dispute. Two papers in the corpus discuss the dispute. A. Aberdein, "Deep Disagreement in Mathematics" (arXiv:2210.16488), states (page 19) that formalization cannot arbitrate a dispute that has to be settled before formalization begins. Y. Lu (arXiv:2603.21074) gives a heuristic argument doubting the main inequality of Corollary 3.12, and the author states that it is not fully justified. C. Zeng, "An Integral-Structure Inflation in the Hull Operation of IUT Corollary 3.12" (hexagon 2610.00646, version 1, 29 October 2026; not on arXiv, so outside this survey and its counts), computes for a Galois extension K of ℚ_p the index of the image of the n-fold tensor power of its ring of integers in the maximal order (Theorem 5.1), and from it draws a conclusion about the passage from Theorem 3.11 to Corollary 3.12 (sections 8 to 10 and 16); the author states that the paper is "not a verdict on IUT". IUT/Audit/TensorOrderIndex.lean and IUT/Audit/TensorOrderIndexMany.lean check the index formula for every number of factors when K/ℚ_p is Galois; for K = ℚ_3(∛3), which is not Galois, the formula fails and the index for two factors is 3^7 (IUT/PaperIV/LocalFields/LocalDiscriminantCubic.lean); see COR312_ANALYSIS.md. The search did not return the manuscript of P. Scholze and J. Stix; the project's account of the dispute is in data/dispute_map.json.

Anabelian and local-field prerequisites. These include:

  • tempered fundamental groups: E. Lepage (arXiv:1004.2150, 0811.3169) and related papers;
  • m-step solvable reconstruction of number fields and local fields: M. Saidi and A. Tamagawa (arXiv:1909.08829), S.-H. Hyeon (arXiv:2405.16950), Y. Mao and M. Saidi (arXiv:2511.05192);
  • two results on outer automorphisms of absolute Galois groups of p-adic fields: K. Kondo (arXiv:2512.09231) for p odd and Y. Nishio (arXiv:2512.05095) for p = 2. Both show that Aut(k) is not normal in Out(G_k) in the cases they treat;
  • the image of a Lubin and Tate logarithm: G. Harbor-Collins (arXiv:2604.27111);
  • canonical models of the four arithmetic (1; ∞) curves: J. Sijsling (arXiv:1707.01158). The author states that IUT I, Definition 3.1 (d) excludes these curves.

abc and Vojta. This theme holds:

  • P. Vojta's formulation of the conjecture for algebraic points with truncated counting functions (arXiv:math/9806171);
  • M. van Frankenhuijsen's proofs of two restricted abc reductions, including Mochizuki's general position reduction (arXiv:2010.09493, Theorem 7.6);
  • elementary coordinates for abc triples, R. Laniewski and K. Müller (arXiv:2610.03212);
  • unconditional bounds weaker than abc (Section 3);
  • many papers that use abc as a hypothesis. These were screened but not read.

Formalization methodology. This theme covers:

  • Lean libraries the project can build on: Mason and Stothers (arXiv:2408.15180), Weierstrass curves (arXiv:2302.10640), local fields (arXiv:2310.01998), q-series and the Jacobi triple product (arXiv:2607.01544);
  • three papers on whether formal statements are faithful to their sources: Formal Conjectures (arXiv:2605.13171), MechAlign (arXiv:2610.35269) and LeanFlow (arXiv:2607.20503).

4. Locations confirmed by two readings

Each item below was reported independently by two readings for this project at the same location. The items describe the text as printed. They are not rulings on the main claims, and the readings differ on what some of them mean for the argument.

  • arXiv:2401.13508 (v4), Corollary 9.11.1.1, page 128. The corollary prints -(1/l*)|LogVol| ≥ -Σ log|q_w^{1/2l}|. As printed, the left side is at most 0 and the right side is positive. arXiv:2403.10430 (v2), Theorem 6.10.1, quotes the corollary in the form -|log q_l| ≤ -(1/l*)|log Vol|.
  • arXiv:2401.13508, Theorem 4.2.2.1 (4), pages 32 to 33. The theorem gives the exponent j². The proof of Theorem 9.9.1, display (9.9.4) on page 121, cites it with j²/l*².
  • arXiv:2401.13508, Corollary 7.4.2, page 57. The lower bound Π|q_w^{1/2l}| lacks the exponent l* of Theorem 7.3.1, page 55.
  • arXiv:2403.10430, Theorem 5.7.1, page 53. The window for the auxiliary prime uses log(2δ log Q). Lemma 5.8.7 (page 56) and the proof of Theorem 7.1.1 (page 73) use log(2δQ).
  • arXiv:2403.10430, proof of Theorem 7.1.1, page 73. The proof uses e*_mod ≤ d_mod. The definitions give e*_mod = 2^12·3^3·5·e_mod, and the inequality does not follow from them.

Two further passages of arXiv:2403.10430 are statements of the paper itself, recorded in its entry in data/literature.json:

  • Theorem 6.10.1 (pages 66 to 67) joins its lower and upper bounds through an equality of hull log-volumes. The proof calls this equality a tautology resting on "the same global normalization".
  • Section 1.6, item 6 (page 21) says that the expected relation between the two theta sets "is not presently established".

5. What the literature offers the project, and what it does not

What it offers:

  • Elementary statements ready for Lean. Examples are the inequality (1/12)(1 + 1/l*) < (1/4)(1 − 1/l) for odd l ≥ 5 used in arXiv:2303.01662, the inequalities 2 log c < log(abc) < 3 log c and the abc and Szpiro implications for Frey curves in arXiv:2610.03212, the height and radical bounds of arXiv:2010.09493 (Lemmas 2.3 and 2.4), and the Frey curve discriminant and conductor relations recalled in arXiv:1705.09251 (Section 3).
  • Infrastructure. Mathlib already holds Polynomial.abc (arXiv:2408.15180), and Lean developments exist for the Weierstrass group law, discrete valuation rings and local fields, and the Jacobi triple product (arXiv:2302.10640, 2310.01998, 2607.01544).
  • Comparison points for the hypotheses of Corollary 3.12. These are initial theta data in standard terms (arXiv:2004.13228), the 6-torsion, 30-torsion and 2-torsion variants (arXiv:2004.13228, 2510.05448), and the four (1; ∞) curves excluded by IUT I, Definition 3.1 (arXiv:1707.01158).
  • Precise statements of what anabelian algorithms can and cannot recover. These include m-step solvable reconstruction (arXiv:1909.08829, 2405.16950, 2511.05192) and the non-normality of Aut(k) in Out(G_k) (arXiv:2512.09231, 2512.05095). They bear on the indeterminacy entries of OPEN_PROBLEMS.md.
  • Checks on faithfulness. Formal Conjectures (arXiv:2605.13171) restricts accepted proofs to the standard axioms and checks them with a comparator; it reports 14 unfaithful statements among 100 reviewed after release. MechAlign (arXiv:2610.35269) reviews the closure of statements a theorem depends on, without proof bodies, and checks that quoted evidence occurs verbatim. LeanFlow (arXiv:2607.20503) freezes statements once they pass a faithfulness check.
  • Weaker abc statements. These give Challenge.ABC intermediate comparison targets: a fixed exponent (arXiv:2611.05217, Theorem B), explicit constants conditional on IUT (arXiv:2503.14510), and unconditional subexponential bounds.

What it does not offer:

  • Among the papers read, the readings for this project found none that constructs the objects of Corollary 3.12 completely enough to state the corollary in Lean with every term defined.
  • Each of the explicit papers states its results as conditional on Corollary 3.12 or on IUT IV.
  • No arXiv paper in the corpus is a line-by-line response to the manuscript of Scholze and Stix, apart from Joshi's own tables (arXiv:2505.10568, Table 2).
  • No formalization of any part of IUT was found on arXiv.

6. Limits of this survey

  • Only arXiv was searched, apart from one supplied working paper. Journal-only papers, manuscripts on personal pages, and papers the search did not return (including the manuscript of Scholze and Stix and the explicit estimates paper cited above) are outside the corpus, except where a paper in the corpus quotes them.
  • The search was retrieved on 2026-11-25. Later versions and later papers are not included.
  • Screening used titles and abstracts only, so 115 included papers are described here only from their abstracts.
  • 35 arXiv papers and the supplied working paper were read in part. Each record lists the sections read, and statements outside them were not checked.
  • Readings used the TeX source or the PDF text. Rendered pages were consulted only for some of the second readings. Internal inconsistencies that only one reading reported are not published here.
  • Relevance scores measure usefulness to this project, not the quality of a paper.
