Formalization dashboard

Generated by scripts/dashboard.py from data/statements.json, data/dependency_graph.json, data/lean_declarations.json and data/countermodels.json. Do not edit by hand; rerun the scripts (see scripts/README.md). Machine-readable form: data/dashboard.json. The statuses behind the counts are written by hand in data/curated/*.json; the scripts check them against fixed rules and against the Lean inventory, not against the papers.

Progress is measured by statements and their statuses, not by lines of Lean or numbers of files. Statement fidelity (is the statement extracted and faithfully stated in Lean?) and proof status (is it proved, and under which hypotheses?) are reported separately.

A statement counts as formalized only when one Lean declaration states its entire content. A Lean lemma that checks one step or one arithmetic piece of a statement is a fragment record of its own (<parent>.frag.<name>); fragments are counted separately, and a parent is never counted as formalized or proved through its fragments.
