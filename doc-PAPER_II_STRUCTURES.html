Structures of IUT II

Source: Mochizuki, Inter-universal Teichmüller Theory II: Hodge-Arakelov-theoretic Evaluation, in the author's manuscript edition. Page numbers are the PDF pages of that manuscript, which equal its printed pages. Displays marked "rendered" were checked against a page render; others come from the text layer only.

Notation: l ≥ 5 is a prime; l^⋇ = (l − 1)/2; |F_l| = F_l/{±1} = {0} ⊔ F_l^⋇ with F_l^⋇ = F_l^×/{±1}; F_l^{⋊±} = F_l ⋊ {±1} (IUT I page 3, rendered; IUT II page 3, rendered). Each j ∈ |F_l| has a unique representative in {0, 1, …, l^⋇} (IUT II page 3). This last fact is proved in IUT/PaperII/Labels.lean (labelRep_bijective).

1. Étale theta functions

  • The étale theta function is the reciprocal of an l-th root of the theta function, written Θ_v at bad places (Introduction, page 2).
  • Group-theoretically, the theta classes are the ι-invariant classes θ(Π) attached to the tempered group Π, constructed up to a μ_{2l}-indeterminacy (Remark 1.12.2 (i), page 58), and the subsets ∞θ(Π) up to μ (Proposition 1.4, Corollary 1.12, pages 55 to 58).
  • In the mono-theta-theoretic version they are attached to a projective system M^Θ_* of mono-theta environments and written θ_env (Definition 2.7, Corollary 2.8, pages 80 to 83).
  • In the base-field-theoretic version they are written θ_bs (Corollary 2.9, pages 84 to 85).

2. Theta values at torsion points

  • Points. For t ∈ LabCusp±, the evaluation section D_{t,μ−} is the decomposition group of the μ_−-translate of the cusp labeled t (Corollary 2.4 (ii), pages 69 to 70). The values depend only on |t| ∈ |F_l| (Corollary 2.5 (ii), page 71).
  • Values. Relative to the standard cyclotomic rigidity and Kummer map, for j ∈ |F_l| the set θ^j consists of the μ_{2l}-orbit of qq_v^{j²}, with j read in {0, 1, …, l^⋇} (Remark 2.5.1 (i), page 72, rendered). Here qq_v (a q with a double underline) is a chosen 2l-th root of the Tate parameter q_v: qq_v = q_v^{1/2l}, determined up to a μ_{2l}-multiple (IUT I, Example 3.2 (iv), page 71, rendered; IUT II page 2, rendered). Relative to q_v itself the theta value is q_v^{j²/(2l)}. The Introduction gives the same list: qq_v^{j²} up to a 2l-th root of unity (page 2, rendered).
  • Relation to the theta series. Remark 2.5.1 (ii), page 73 (rendered): the reciprocals of the l-th powers of these theta values (not the values themselves) correspond to the values Θ̈(±√−1 · q_X^{j/2}) of the theta series of [EtTh], Proposition 1.4, i.e. to values at points separated by periods from ±√−1.
  • Up to what. Each value is a μ_{2l}-orbit (θ) or μ-orbit (∞θ) (Corollary 2.5 (ii), page 71). The μ_{2l}-indeterminacies for different j are independent of each other, not synchronized (Remark 2.5.1 (iii), page 73). A value-profile chooses one element of each orbit; the set of value-profiles has cardinality (2l)^{l^⋇} (Corollary 3.5 (ii), page 94, rendered).
  • Zero label. θ^0 is the μ_{2l}-orbit of the identity, which gives the zero-labeled splittings (Corollary 2.6 (ii), page 76).
  • Why j² and not other powers. Remark 2.6.3 (pages 79 to 80, rendered) motivates the subgraph Γ_Y^▶ with vertices 0, …, l^⋇ via the quantity ‖Γ′‖. See data/curated/iut2.json, record IUT2.Rem2_6_3, for an interpretation issue on page 80.

3. Kummer-theoretic representations

  • Kummer isomorphisms relate Frobenioid-theoretic monoids to group-theoretic ones: (Ψ_{†C_v})_t ⥲ Ψ_cns(M^Θ_*)_t, with an inner indeterminacy independent of t (Corollary 3.6 (i), page 99).
  • The chain Ψ_{†F^Θ_v} ⥲ Ψ_env ⥲ Ψ_gau ⥲ Ψ_{F_gau} combines Kummer theory and Galois evaluation (Corollary 3.6 (ii), pages 100 to 101; Propositions 4.2 and 4.4, pages 123 to 131).
  • At good nonarchimedean v the units admit a unique Ẑ^×-orbit of isomorphisms (Proposition 4.2 (ii), page 124); at archimedean v a unique {±1}-orbit (Proposition 4.4 (ii), page 129).
  • A ×-Kummer structure is a Ẑ^×-orbit of isomorphisms O^×(G) ⥲ O^×(A); a ×μ-Kummer structure is an Ism-orbit of isomorphisms O^{×μ}(G) ⥲ O^{×μ}(A) (Definition 4.9 (i), pages 153 to 154).
  • Kummer N-th power maps respect multiplication but not addition (Remark 3.6.4, pages 105 to 108). Only the first power of the theta function crosses the mono-theta "narrow bridge".

4. Gaussian monoids

  • Mono-theta-theoretic: Ψ_ξ = Ψ^×_cns⟨F_l^⋇⟩ · ξ^ℕ and ∞Ψ_ξ = Ψ^×_cns⟨F_l^⋇⟩ · ξ^{Q≥0}, for value-profiles ξ; Ψ_{2l·ξ} does not depend on ξ (Corollary 3.5 (ii), pages 94 to 95, rendered 94).
  • Formal version at good v: Ψ_gau = Ψ^×_cns⟨F_l^⋇⟩ × {R≥0 · (…, j² · log(p_v), …)}, inside ∏_{j ∈ F_l^⋇} Ψ^ss_cns,j, with j ∈ {1, …, l^⋇}; formal evaluation sends log(p_v)·log(Θ) to (…, j² log(p_v), …) (Proposition 4.1 (iv), page 122, rendered).
  • Global: D_gau is the weighted diagonal (1²φ, 2²φ, …, j²φ, …) (Remark 4.5.4, page 136, rendered); volumes at each j get weight 1/l^⋇ (Remark 4.7.3 (iii), page 145, rendered).
  • Gaussian monoids built group-theoretically are uniradial, since they use the cyclotomic rigidity of Corollary 1.11 (b) (Corollary 3.7 (ii), page 111).
  • Elementary arithmetic (not in the paper, proved in IUT/PaperII/Labels.lean): 24 · Σ_{j=1}^{l^⋇} j² = l(l² − 1), and (1/l^⋇) · Σ j² = l(l + 1)/12.

6. Indeterminacies from changes of representation

  • Conjugacy: Π_v-conjugacy at nonarchimedean v (Corollary 4.5 (i), pages 131 to 132).
  • Conjugate synchronization: for F_l^{⋊±}-symmetry the inner indeterminacy is independent of |t| (Corollary 3.5 (i), page 94, rendered; Corollary 3.6 (i), page 99).
  • F_l^⋇-symmetry: independent conjugacy indeterminacies for distinct j, plus one j-independent indeterminacy (Corollary 4.7 (ii), pages 140 to 141).
  • Unit isomorphisms: Ẑ^× (good nonarchimedean), {±1} (archimedean), Ism for ×μ-Kummer structures (Proposition 4.2, Proposition 4.4, Definition 4.9 (i)).
  • Theta values: μ_{2l} per label, independent across labels (Remark 2.5.1 (iii), page 73).
  • Prime-strips at bad v: automorphisms of the split Frobenioids inducing the identity on the F^{⊢×}-prime-strip, absorbed by full poly-isomorphisms (Remark 4.10.1, pages 161 to 162).
  • Global realified Frobenioids: compatibility only up to R>0-orbits (Corollary 4.10 (v), pages 160 to 161).
  • Representative of a label: the identification of label 0 with F_l^⋇ (assignment "0, ≻ ↦ >") when passing to the D-ΘNF-Hodge theater (Remark 3.8.2 (ii), page 116; Corollary 4.10 (i), page 158, rendered).
