IUTLean

A Lean 4 formalization and verification project for the four papers on inter-universal Teichmüller theory.

The project formalizes what the papers state, proves only what is justified, and records precisely what remains unproved. It assumes neither that the theory is correct nor that it is incorrect. It does not contain a proof of the abc conjecture.
