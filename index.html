Inter-universal Teichmüller Theory in Lean

A Lean 4 project on Shinichi Mochizuki's Inter-universal Teichmüller Theory I to IV. It catalogs every numbered statement of the four papers, states in Lean what can be stated without inventing definitions, and proves what it can with the kernel as the only judge. Every step that cannot yet be stated or proved is recorded as an open obligation with its source page.

blocked by missing justification (BLOCKED_BY_MISSING_JUSTIFICATION): the text gives an assertion where a formalization needs a derivation; this is not a claim that the result is false.

The abc conjecture is not proved here, and IUT III, Corollary 3.12 is not formalized. The project takes no side in the dispute about the theory: it neither assumes that the theory is correct nor that it is wrong. A countermodel in this repository refutes a simplified abstraction stated in Lean; it is not a counterexample to a statement of the papers.

Where the work stops

Nothing in the library constructs the objects of IUT I to III. Not done: Hodge theaters, prime strips, the Θ-link and the log-link, the log-theta-lattice, log-shells and tensor packets with their normalized log-volumes, the indeterminacies (Ind1) to (Ind3), the holomorphic hull of possible images, a Lean statement of Theorem 3.11 or of Corollary 3.12, the comparison of Corollary 3.12 stated on the papers' objects, and the specialization of IUT IV, Theorem A to abc triples.

The module IUT.PaperIII.Corollary312.Comparison is an abstract interface: it takes the regions as inputs (a measure, a hull map, a q-pilot region, a family of possible images), states the comparison as a region containment and as a log-volume inequality on those given regions, and proves the conditional passage to the numerical layer. Its objects are not the papers' objects.

What is done is arithmetic and order-theoretic: numerical consequences of stated inequalities, elementary estimates of IUT IV, countermodels to simplified abstractions, and models of the arguments in the published discussion. Three statement records of the papers are proved in full in Lean, all in IUT IV, Sections 1 and 2:

  • IUT IV, Proposition 1.6 (The Prime Number Theorem), Section 1, page 16: IUT.PaperIV.Proposition1_6.proposition1_6
  • IUT IV, Proposition 1.7 (Weighted Averages), Section 1, pages 16 to 17: IUT.PaperIV.Proposition1_7.weighted_averages
  • IUT IV, Proposition 2.1 (Well-known Estimates), Section 2, page 40: IUT.PaperIV.Proposition2_1.proposition2_1

Kernel acceptance establishes that a Lean declaration is proved as written. Whether it says what the paper says is a separate, human comparison against the cited pages; the procedure page says what each check establishes and what it does not.

A fragment is a Lean lemma that checks one step or one arithmetic piece of a statement of the papers. It is counted on its own; its parent statement is not counted as formalized or proved through it. Formalized in full means that one Lean declaration states the entire content of the record; proved in full means that, in addition, the declaration is a theorem accepted with the allowed axioms. Unauthorized axioms and placeholders are counted over the 47 modules of the compiled inventory data/lean_declarations.json.
