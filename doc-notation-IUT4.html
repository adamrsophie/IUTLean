Section 3: species and mutations (pages 67 to 87)

Universe and set-theory conventions (pages 67 to 68)

  • ZFC-model. A model of the ZFC axioms ("sets" and ∈).
  • **Grothendieck universe V.** A set with: (i) transitive; (ii) ℕ ∈ V; (iii) closed under power set; (iv) closed under union of members; (v) closed under surjective images of members. A V-set is an element of V.
  • **Existence axiom (†^G). Every set lies in some universe. A ZFC-model satisfying it is a ZFCG-model**. Consequences: any set-indexed family of universes lies in a larger universe; infinite ascending chains V_0 ∈ V_1 ∈ … exist; infinite descending chains do not (foundation).
  • Absoluteness proviso. Every set-theoretic formula used is absolute: its truth for sets in V relative to V is equivalent to its truth relative to any universe W with V ∈ W.
  • The paper does not settle whether ZFCG-models exist; it points to Feferman ([Ffmn]).

None of the Section 3 notions is formalized. In Lean 4 with Mathlib the closest analogues are CategoryTheory.Category in a universe Type u and functors between such categories; Lean's universe hierarchy Type 0 : Type 1 : … plays the role of the chain of universes, and ULift the role of passing to a larger universe. This is a comparison, not a formalization.
