Notation dictionary for IUT III

Source: Mochizuki, Inter-universal Teichmüller Theory III. Each entry gives the symbol, a short paraphrase, and the page where it is defined or first introduced in this paper.

  • "(r)" marks an entry checked against a rendered page.
  • "(t)" marks an entry read from the extracted text only.
  • Symbols defined in IUT I or IUT II are listed with the citation the paper gives. They were not read in those papers for this dictionary.

Indices, places and labels

  • V, V^bad, V^good, V^non, V^arc: places of K from the initial Θ-data (IUTchI Def 3.1). Used throughout, e.g. page 24 (r).
  • V_Q, v_Q: places of Q; v | v_Q means v lies over v_Q. Page 89 (r), Rem 2.4.2 (ii).
  • V^bad ≠ ∅: recalled in the statement of Cor 3.12, page 174 (r).
  • F_l, |F_l|, F*_l: labels. |F_l| has the labels 0, ..., l* and F*_l has the labels 1, ..., l*. Used in Thm 3.11 (i), pages 153 to 154 (r). Defined in IUTchI.
  • l*: the largest label in F*_l; the theta values are q^{j²}, j = 1, ..., l* (Fig. 3.4, page 173 (r)). Defined in IUTchI; its formula was not read here.
  • S^±_{j+1}: the index set of the capsule of the procession used at label j; its log-shells carry the labels {0, 1, ..., j} (Rem 3.9.3, page 120 (r)). Prop 3.4 (ii), pages 101 to 103 (t); used in Thm 3.11 (i)(a), page 153 (r).
  • (n,m) ∈ Z × Z: lattice position. Def 1.4, page 45 (r).
  • n,◦: vertically coric object of the n-th column. Thm 1.5 (i), page 48 (r); Thm 3.11, page 153 (r).
  • △: the result of identifying the labels 0 and ⟨F*_l⟩. Recalled in Cor 3.12, page 174 (r), citing IUTchII Cor 4.10 (i).
  • ≻, >: labels of D-prime-strips in a Θ^{±ell}NF-Hodge theater. Thm 1.5 (iii), pages 48 to 49 (r), citing IUTchI.

Prime-strips and Hodge theaters

  • n,m HT^{Θ±ellNF}: Θ^{±ell}NF-Hodge theater at (n,m). Def 1.4, page 45 (r). Defined in IUTchI Def 6.13.
  • n,◦HT^{D-Θ±ellNF}: the D-Θ^{±ell}NF-Hodge theater determined by the n-th column. Thm 3.11, page 153 (r).
  • 𝔉, 𝔇, 𝔇^⊢: F-, D- and D^⊢-prime-strips. Prop 1.2 (x), page 34 (r). Defined in IUTchI.
  • 𝔉^{⊢×μ}, 𝔉^{⊢×}: unit portions. Def 1.1 (iv), page 27 (r); Thm 1.5 (iii), page 49 (r).
  • 𝔉^⊩, 𝔉^{⊩⊥}, 𝔉^{⊩▶}: F^⊩-prime-strips and the split-monoid sub- and quotient versions. Def 2.4, pages 87 to 89 (t; page 89 r).
  • 𝔉^{⊩▶×μ}: the prime-strips related by the Θ^{×μ}_LGP-link. Def 3.8 (ii), page 113 (r).
  • F^{⊢×μ}_△(n,m D^⊢_△): bi-coric F^{⊢×μ}-prime-strip. Thm 1.5 (iii), page 49 (r).
  • Prc(n,◦D^⊢_T): the procession of D^⊢-prime-strips. Thm 3.11 (i), page 153 (r), citing IUTchI Prop 6.9 (ii).

Links

  • †𝔉 →^{log} ‡𝔉: log-link. The tautological log-link is post-composed with a poly-isomorphism. It is called the full log-link when the poly-isomorphism is full. Def 1.1 (i), (iii), pages 24 and 27 (r).
  • Θ^{×μ}, Θ^{×μ}_gau: horizontal links of the (Gaussian) log-theta-lattice. Def 1.4, page 45 (r). Defined in IUTchII Cor 4.10 (iii).
  • Θ^{×μ}_LGP, Θ^{×μ}_lgp: full poly-isomorphisms †𝔉^{⊩▶×μ}_{LGP or lgp} ≅ *𝔉^{⊩▶×μ}_△, relative to a log-link ‡HT → †HT. Def 3.8 (ii), page 113 (r).

Log-shells and packets

  • p*_v: p_v if p_v is odd, p_v² if p_v is even. Def 1.1 (i), page 24 (r).
  • I_{†F_v}: log-shell. At nonarchimedean v it is (p*_v)^{-1} times the pre-log-shell, the image of the G_v-invariants of the units. Def 1.1 (i), page 24 (r). The archimedean version is in Def 1.1 (ii), page 25 (r).
  • log(†F_v), log(†𝔉): the codomain Frobenioid or topological module of the log-link. Def 1.1, pages 24, 25 and 27 (r).
  • I_{†𝔇^⊢_v} = I(†G_v) ⊆ k~(†G_v): archimedean mono-analytic log-shell. Prop 1.2 (vii), pages 32 to 33 (t; page 33 r). The nonarchimedean version is Prop 1.2 (vi), page 32 (t).
  • log(^A F_{v_Q}) = ⊗_{α∈A} log(^α F_{v_Q}): local holomorphic tensor packet. Prop 3.1, pages 92 to 93 (t).
  • I^Q(−): Q-span of the integral structure of a packet. Prop 3.1, Prop 3.2 (ii), pages 92 to 99 (t). Used on pages 115 to 117 (r).
  • I(S^±_{j+1}; n,◦D^⊢_{v_Q}), I(S^±_{j+1},j; n,◦D^⊢_v): mono-analytic integral structures for capsule S^±_{j+1}. Thm 3.11 (i)(a), page 153 (r).
  • I^Q(^A F_{V_Q}) = ∏_{v_Q} I^Q(^A F_{v_Q}): global packet. Prop 3.9 (iii), page 117 (r).

Monoids, fields and Frobenioids

  • Ψ_cns(−): constant monoids of a prime-strip. Thm 1.5, page 49 (r). Defined in IUTchII.
  • Ψ_{F_LGP}, Ψ_LGP, ∞Ψ: LGP-monoids. Prop 3.4 (ii), pages 101 to 103 (t); Prop 3.5 (ii)(c), page 105 (r).
  • Ψ^⊥_{F_LGP}(−)_v ⊆ Ψ_{F_LGP}(−)_v: splitting monoid at v ∈ V^bad. Prop 3.5 (ii)(c), page 105 (r).
  • Ψ^⊥_{F_lgp}(−)_v: defined to be Ψ^⊥_{F_LGP}(−)_v. Def 3.8 (i), page 112 (r).
  • M̄_MOD = M̄_mod, (M_mod)_α: copies of the number field. Thm 3.11 (i)(c), page 154 (r); Prop 3.3, page 99 (t).
  • F_MOD, F_mod, F^R_MOD, F^R_mod: global non-realified and realified Frobenioids. Example 3.6 (page 106) and Prop 3.7 (pages 109 to 112), text; used on page 154 (r).
  • C^⊩_LGP, C^⊩_lgp, C^⊩_△: global realified Frobenioid portions. Prop 3.7, page 112 (r); Def 3.8 (i), page 112 (r).
  • D^⊩(n,m D^⊢_△): bi-coric mono-analytic global realified Frobenioid. Thm 1.5 (v), page 50 (r).
  • q_v (displayed with a double underline as q): the q-parameter at v ∈ V^bad. Def 3.8 (i), page 112 (r), citing IUTchI Example 3.2 (iv).
  • r_v, r^▶_v: weights. r_v = [(F_mod)_v : Q_{v_Q}]·log(p_v) at good v and [(F_mod)_v : Q_{v_Q}]·ord_v(q_v)·log(p_v) at bad v; r^▶_v = −r_v / Σ_{w∈V^bad} r_w. Rem 2.4.2 (ii), page 89 (r).

Pilot objects

  • Θ-pilot object: the object of ∏_{j∈F*_l} (F_MOD)_j or ∏_j (F_mod)_j, or its realification, determined by generators up to torsion of Ψ^⊥_{F_lgp}(HT)_v, v ∈ V^bad. Def 3.8 (i), page 112 (r).
  • q-pilot object: the object of C^⊩_△ determined by generators up to torsion of the splitting monoid of F^⊢_{△,v}, i.e. by the q_v. Def 3.8 (i), page 112 (r).

Log-volumes

  • M(−): the set of nonempty compact open subsets (nonarchimedean), or of compact closures of nonempty open subsets (archimedean). Prop 3.9 (i), pages 115 to 116 (r).
  • μ^log_{α,v_Q}, μ^log_{A,v_Q}, μ^log_{A,α,v}: packet-normalized log-volumes M(I^Q(−)) → R. Prop 3.9 (i), page 115 (r).
    • Integral structures have log-volume 0.
    • At nonarchimedean places, multiplication by p_v adds −log(p_v).
    • At archimedean places, multiplication by e adds 1.
  • Procession-normalization: the average over the capsules of a procession. Prop 3.9 (i), page 116 (r). Cor 3.12 states that the average is over j ∈ F*_l (pages 173 and 181 (r)), so the factor is 1/l*; the capsules of Prop 3.4 (ii) carry the labels j ∈ {1, ..., l*} = F*_l (page 102 (r)). Page 183 (r) speaks of a normalization "with respect to j ∈ |F_l|" without a factor.
  • μ^log_{A,V_Q}: global log-volume, the sum over v_Q. Prop 3.9 (iii), page 117 (r).
  • Sub(M), Fn(M), Rss(M): pre-ample subsets, finite-valued Borel functions, and the quotient by integration. Rem 3.9.4 (i), page 120 (r).
  • log(q) = ord_v(q_v)·log(p_v). Rem 3.9.4 (iv), page 124 (r).
  • −|log(Θ)| ∈ R ∪ {+∞}: procession-normalized mono-analytic log-volume of the holomorphic hull of the union of possible images of a Θ-pilot object, subject to (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3). Cor 3.12, pages 173 to 174 (r).
  • −|log(q)| ∈ R: the log-volume of the image of a q-pilot object, not subject to the indeterminacies. Cor 3.12, page 174 (r).
  • C_Θ: any real number with −|log(Θ)| ≤ C_Θ·|log(q)|. Cor 3.12, page 174 (r).
  • R_{≤−|log(Θ)|} = {λ ∈ R | λ ≤ −|log(Θ)|}. Cor 3.12 Step (xi-d), page 183 (r).

Hulls

  • Holomorphic hull: the smallest λ·O containing U, with every component of λ nonzero, for relatively compact U; otherwise the whole I^Q(−). Rem 3.9.5 (i), page 127 (r).
  • ℙ, ℍ, φ: direct product regions, hulls (proper subsets), and the hull map ℙ → ℍ with (P1) to (P3). Rem 3.9.5 (ii), page 127 (r).
  • Φ(P), Ξ(P), H_{Φ(P)}, H_{Ξ(P)}: hull-approximants. Rem 3.9.5 (iii), (iv), pages 128 to 129 (t; page 129 r).
  • n,◦U, n,◦U^Q, n,◦Ū: unions of possible images, their ambient packets, and their hulls, indexed by j ∈ |F_l|, v_Q ∈ V_Q (an indexing of sets, not the averaging index). Cor 3.12 proof, page 174 (r).

Indeterminacies and properties

  • (Ind1): induced by automorphisms of the procession. Thm 3.11 (i), page 154 (r).
  • (Ind2): independent copies of Ism (nonarchimedean) or of order 2 automorphisms (archimedean) on each direct summand. Thm 3.11 (i), page 154 (r).
  • (Ind3): upper semi-compatibility of the packet isomorphisms along a column. Thm 3.11 (ii), page 156 (r).
  • Ism: the poly-automorphism group of Prop 1.2 (vi). Page 32 (t). Cited on page 154 (r).
  • IPL, SHE, APT, HIS: input prime-strip link, simultaneous holomorphic expressibility, algorithmic parallel transport, hidden internal structures. Rem 3.11.1 (iii), (iv), pages 160 to 162 (t). Recalled in Cor 3.12 Step (xi-b), page 182 (r).
  • Kummer-detachment and étale-transport indeterminacies: Rem 1.5.4 (i), page 56 (t).
