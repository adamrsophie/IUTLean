Proof attempts

A log of what was attempted in Lean and how it ended, limited to what the repository shows. Each entry names the module and the main declarations, the statement attempted, the assumptions, and the outcome. Outcomes are "proved" (no hypothesis beyond the statement, allowed axioms only), "proved under stated hypotheses", or "not finished". A Lean lemma that checks only one step of a statement of the papers proves that step, not the statement: such a lemma is a fragment (docs/DECISIONS.md, decision 12), and "proved" below then refers to the fragment. Approaches that were abandoned are in docs/FAILED_APPROACHES.md. Current counts are in docs/DASHBOARD.md.

abc statements

  • Equivalence of the three forms. IUT/Applications/ABC.lean: abc_iff_abcFinite, abc_iff_abcLog, rad_eq_radical, coprime_iff_pairwise, restated in Solution.lean and checked by lake comparator. Proved.
  • Agreement with the Formal Conjectures statement. IUT/Applications/ABCFormalConjectures.lean: abc_iff_ABC, ltConstantMul_iff_ABC, qualityVariant_iff_ABC. Proved. The Formal Conjectures statement is restated locally, not imported; see docs/EXTERNAL_PROJECTS.md.

IUT IV

  • Local estimates, Propositions 1.2 to 1.7. IUT/PaperIV/Proposition1_2.lean to Proposition1_7.lean. Where a statement needs objects that are not formalized (local field theory, tensor products of local fields, metrized real vector spaces), the module proves the numerical content and the missing properties enter as real-number hypotheses; each docstring says which. The docstring of Proposition1_5.lean states that it is not a formalization of Proposition 1.5. Proposition 1.6 is proved from Mathlib's prime number theorem with η_prm ≥ 2.
  • Proposition 2.1. IUT/PaperIV/Proposition2_1.lean, read with p ∉ A from the render. Proved.
  • Definition 1.9 and arithmetic divisors. IUT/PaperIV/ArithmeticDivisor.lean, Definition1_9.lean: ADiv, degWeight, degE with its basic properties (degE_add, degE_nonneg, degE_eq_normalizedDeg). Proved; the pull-back to an extension is not defined (OPEN_PROBLEMS.md, P-PULL).
  • Theorem 1.10. IUT/PaperIV/Theorem1_10/: Steps (ii), (iii), (v), (viii), the elementary facts, and final_inequality. Each step is proved under the hypotheses the paper uses, including the inequality of IUT III Corollary 3.12 where the paper invokes it. Two hypotheses the page does not write (D ≥ 0 in Step (iii), nonnegativity in Step (v)) are explicit in Lean.
  • Corollaries 2.2 and 2.3. IUT/PaperIV/Corollary2_2/Arithmetic.lean, IUT/PaperIV/Corollary2_3.lean: the arithmetic of conditions (P1) to (P7), P1_l_ge_seven, and the extension to ε > 1 (BDle_mono_eps). Proved under stated hypotheses; graded conditional in docs/PAPER_IV_DERIVATION.md because they depend on Theorem 1.10.
  • **Theorem A to Challenge.ABCLog.** Not attempted in Lean: heights, log-differents and log-conductors on curves are not in Mathlib (OPEN_PROBLEMS.md, P-HT).
  • Grothendieck universes (§3). IUT/Foundations/GrothendieckUniverse.lean: exists_universe_forall_mem, exists_ascending_chain (both from the hypothesis ExistenceAxiom), not_exists_descending_mem_chain. Proved. ExistenceAxiom is independent of what Lean proves about ZFSet.{u}, so the two conditional results are vacuous in models where it fails (module docstring).

IUT III

  • Corollary 3.12, numerical layer. IUT/PaperIII/Corollary312/Numerical.lean: inequality_of_nonneg, constantForm_of_top, conclusion_iff_constantForm, stepXIf_inclusion_iff_inequality, thm1_10_final_of_cor312. Proved. This is not a formalization of Corollary 3.12 (docs/DECISIONS.md, decision 6).
  • Remark 3.9.5, the hull operator. IUT/PaperIII/Remark3_9_5/HullOperator.lean: IsHullMap, IsHullMap.unique, IsHullMap.eq_sInter. Proved: the intersection formula follows from (P1) to (P3) alone.
  • An interface for the last step of Corollary 3.12. IUT/PaperIII/Corollary312/Comparison.lean. Attempted: the comparison of the q-pilot region with the hull of the union of possible images, as a containment (Containment) and as a log-volume inequality (LogVolumeComparison), and the passage to the numerical layer (conclusion_of_containment, conclusion_of_procContainment). Assumptions: the comparison itself, μ(q-pilot) ≠ 0 and < 1, and finiteness; all regions are inputs. Outcome: proved conditionally; examples on ℝ (example_bridge_hypotheses, example_comparison_fails, example_union_matters). This is an interface, not the papers' objects.
  • Theorem 3.11 and Corollary 3.12 themselves. Not attempted; the statements cannot be written without the missing definitions (docs/DECISIONS.md, decision 4).

IUT I and IUT II

  • Poly-morphisms and capsules (IUT I §0). IUT/PaperI/PolyMorphism.lean: fullPolyIso, PolyMorphism.comp, isPolyIso_fullPolyIso, IsPolyIso.comp, PolyMorphism.comp_assoc, Capsule, Capsule.Hom. Definitions of pages 33 and 34 with elementary lemmas, proved. The category laws for capsules are not proved.
  • Initial Θ-data, partial. IUT/PaperI/InitialThetaData.lean: PartialInitialThetaConditions, not_sqrtNegOneMem_rat, auxiliaryPrimeFirstClause_five, AuxiliaryPrimeFirstClause.coprime_six, TorsionRational.of_dvd, TorsionRational.two_of_six. Proved. Conditions (d) to (f) are not stated.
  • Labels of IUT II. IUT/PaperII/Labels.lean: labelRep_bijective, gammaNormValue_strictMono, and the identity 24·Σ j² = l(l² − 1). Proved. The strict monotonicity is what the literal reading of Remark 2.6.3 (d) gives; see OPEN_PROBLEMS.md, A29.

Prerequisites

IUT/Prerequisites/PositiveRationalsZhatUnits.lean (posRat_inter_zhatUnits, eq_one_of_forall_valuation_eq_one) and IUT/Prerequisites/ProductFormula.lean (prod_normAbs_eq_one, sum_log_normAbs_eq_zero). Proved.

The published discussion

  • Scholze and Stix. IUT/Audit/ScholzeStix/. Attempted: the reduction of their Section 1.2 from Claim 6 to Vojta's inequality (vojta_of_claim6); the averaging identity (avgSq_eq); the two displays of their §1.3 (consequence_with_squares, consequence_without_squares, restated in Solution.lean); the consistency of a cycle of scalars (Diagram.lean, not_consistent_square) and the tolerance condition for a cycle with the averaged scalar (blur_bound, consistentUpTo_avgSq_iff). Assumptions: Claim 6, (1.2), (1.3) and N ≥ 0 as hypotheses of the reduction; the hypothesis (Lin) (every identification is a positive scalar) and the chosen tolerance model ConsistentUpTo for the diagram files. Outcome: proved under these assumptions. The lower bound on the tolerance B in consistentUpTo_avgSq_iff is the tolerance hypothesis evaluated, not a derived bound.
  • Mochizuki's responses. IUT/Audit/MochizukiResponses/. Attempted: the examples (LVEx) (not_linear, LVExMeasure.volume_R, LVExMeasure.gluing), (SSIdEx) (SSIdEx.not_iso_of_ne_one) and the toy models (ToyModels.stepXIf_formal). Assumptions: the regions and admissibility conditions on λ of the report; in gluing the comparison λ < μ^log(S) follows from those conditions alone. Outcome: proved; each models a passage of the reports, not a statement of the papers.

Countermodels

IUT/Countermodels/: eight modules of models in which a simplified abstraction of a step of Corollary 3.12 fails when a compatibility condition is dropped, together with positive statements under the condition. Attempted statement: each abstraction listed in docs/COUNTERMODELS.md. Assumptions: those of each Lean theorem as listed there (for the log-volume shifts, sets of finite positive measure and a nonzero dilation; for the comparison model, positive pilot lengths). Outcome: each abstraction is refuted in a model; scope: the abstraction only. The list, with the question each answers, is in docs/COUNTERMODELS.md. None refutes a statement of the papers.
