External formalization projects

This file records existing formal work related to the four IUT papers and to the abc conjecture, what it claims, what it actually states in Lean, and what it trusts. Machine-readable data: data/external_projects.json. Everything here was obtained on 2026-11-25 by reading the code at the commits listed and by grepping. No external project was built: their toolchains (Lean v4.32.0, v4.33.1) differ from ours (v4.36.0), and building them would require their own Mathlib. Axiom claims of the external projects are therefore their own claims, not re-checked by our kernel; our trust inventory is textual.

Primary source pages read for the comparison: IUT III, pages 153 to 159 (Theorem 3.11 and its proof) and pages 173 to 174 (statement of Corollary 3.12 and the start of its proof). The displays of pages 153, 154, 156, 173 and 174 were checked against rendered page images.

2. lana-agents/iut

URL: https://github.com/lana-agents/iut. Commit b4a9966ffe09b5019b57e05b2497972b22799e4a (2026-11-22). Apache-2.0. Lean v4.32.0, Mathlib tag v4.32.0 (revision 81a5d257). 768 Lean files (752 in Iut/, 6 in Iut4Sec1/, comparator and script files), about 234,000 lines, plus 15 pinned dependencies of the lana-agents organization (about 4,400 Lean files, 1.26 million lines; section 2.6). Its formalization.yaml records that the code was produced with automated (machine-generated) assistance, that its review status is "agent-reviewed", and that it "has not been reviewed by a human".

2.1 What it claims

The README (all 1102 lines read) and formalization.yaml (read in full) say plainly that the repository does not verify IUT and does not prove abc. The main theorem is a conditional implication:

def Iut.Cor312VariantHolds : Prop :=
  ∀ D : InitialThetaData.{0}, Corollary312Variant (concreteVariantData D)
theorem Iut.classicalABC_of_variant (h312 : Cor312VariantHolds) : ClassicalABC

(Iut/Concrete/ThetaRegion.lean:375, Iut/MainTheorem.lean:36), with claimed axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. The variant is called "project-owner-specified", is "deliberately not identified with the published Corollary 3.12", and is never proved or assumed as an axiom. A comparator challenge (Comparator/Challenge.lean) states Cor312VariantHolds → ABC with the Formal Conjectures statement ABC.abc copied from their commit 1646ca16 (the file at that commit is byte-identical to the one at 9097e2f9 that we use).

Other claims: Theorem 1.10 of IUT IV from the variant (Iut.Theorem110Invariants.theorem110), IUT IV Corollaries 2.2 and 2.3, [GenEll] Theorem 2.1 (ii) implies (i) for the tripod, the identification of the tempered group at the bad places with André's tempered group, Theorem B for the Tate curves of the Θ-data, and a large body of supporting number theory (Serre's different bound, torsion of Legendre curves, stable reduction, Chebyshev bounds). Recorded deviations: Proposition 1.6 of IUT IV is used with factor 3/2 in place of the printed 4/3; reduction predicates up to change of variables; the cyclic subgroup bound of [GenEll] Lemma 3.5 weakened at the prime 2; two local statements restricted to packets over p.

2.2 How Corollary 3.12 is stated

The chain of definitions, unfolded until Mathlib objects are reached:

  • Iut.Corollary312Variant X := X.qPilot.lhs ≤ X.rhsData.rhs (Iut/Cor312/Statement.lean:89), for a bundle Corollary312VariantData of D : InitialThetaData, QPilotData D, RHSData D (Statement.lean:68).
  • Left side (Iut/Cor312/LeftHandSide.lean:81 to 93): lhs = −absLogQ, absLogQ = logQ / (2ℓ), logQ = Σ_{w ∈ V(F)^bad} weight w · ord_w(q_w) · log p_w. In the concrete instance (ThetaRegion.lean:348) weight w = f_w / [F : ℚ] and q_w is the Tate parameter of E at w (defined from j(E) in tate-curves-theta). This is a real number computed from the curve; the factor 1/2ℓ is taken from the proof of IUT IV, Theorem 1.10.
  • Right side (Iut/Cor312/RightHandSide.lean:99, 107): rhs = processionVol (hull (thetaPilot)), the unweighted average over the ℓ* = (ℓ − 1)/2 capsules (Iut/Cor312/LogVolume.lean:207) of global log-volumes of the holomorphic hull of a region in the container of tensor packets ⊗_j K_{v_j} (Mathlib PiTensorProduct of completions, with normalized Haar measure).
  • The region (ThetaRegion.lean:170 to 183, 273): at a prime p and tuple c of places of V ≅ V_mod, ⋃_{φ ∈ indAut} φ(q_{v_j}^{j²} · ⊗_l 𝓘_{v_l}), where q_v is a 2ℓ-th root of the Tate parameter, 𝓘_v the log-shell (2p)⁻¹ log(𝒪_v^×) (from padic-log-volume), and indAut = tensorAut (Iut/Concrete/LocalConstruct/IndAut.lean:219, 299) is the set of tensor products ⊗ g_j of arbitrary ℚ_p-linear automorphisms g_j of K_{v_j} that preserve the lattice 𝓘_{v_j}. At the archimedean place: the unit ball of a tensor product Hermitian metric with sign and conjugation automorphisms. The label is {j} (ivLabels, line 237); a "literal" version over all labels, Cor312LiteralHolds (line 383), is implied by the variant (Iut/Concrete/Literal.lean:115).
  • InitialThetaData (Iut/Cor312/ThetaData/Basic.lean:81) is a structure of genuine data: a number field F, an algebraic closure, a WeierstrassCurve with IsElliptic, a set of bad places of F_mod, and fields stating the conditions of IUT I, Definition 3.1, about Mathlib objects and about objects of the dependencies (pi1, tempered-fundamental-groups, orbicurve-cores).

So every term of Cor312VariantHolds is defined. It is not a statement about abstract parameters: it is one concrete real inequality per initial Θ-datum.

2.3 Comparison with the original Corollary 3.12

IUT III, Corollary 3.12 (pages 173 to 174), in our reading: in the situation of Theorem 3.11 (initial Θ-data and a collection of distinct Θ^{±ell}NF-Hodge theaters arising from an LGP-Gaussian log-theta-lattice), let −|log(Θ)| ∈ ℝ ∪ {+∞} be the procession-normalized mono-analytic log-volume of the holomorphic hull of the union of the possible images of a Θ-pilot object, relative to the Kummer isomorphisms of Theorem 3.11 (ii), in the multiradial representation of Theorem 3.11 (i), subject to (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3); let −|log(q)| ∈ ℝ be the corresponding log-volume of the image of a q-pilot object, not subject to the indeterminacies, with |log(q)| > 0. Then −|log(Θ)| ∈ ℝ and −|log(Θ)| ≥ −|log(q)|.

Differences, each read off the code:

  1. Variant, not the original. The inequality direction is the same (−|log q| ≤ −|log Θ|), but −|log Θ| is not defined as in the paper. In the paper it is the log-volume of a hull of possible images of the Θ-pilot under Kummer isomorphisms in the multiradial representation of Theorem 3.11; in lana-agents/iut it is the log-volume of a region written down directly from D (theta values q^{j²} acting on log-shells, closed under a chosen automorphism group). No Hodge theater, Θ-link, Kummer isomorphism, or multiradial algorithm enters the definition of concreteVariantData. The repository says the relation of this region to the paper's region "is documented per-module but not itself a theorem" (Iut/Cor312/Statement.lean, docstring).
  2. (Ind2) is enlarged from independent copies of Ism (IUT III, Proposition 1.2 (vi)) to all lattice automorphisms of each factor log-shell; documented as making the region larger.
  3. (Ind1) is read as in IUT IV, proof of Theorem 1.10, Steps (iv) to (v) (label j only), not as the union over the label permutations; the literal reading is a separate, weaker hypothesis.
  4. (Ind3) is modeled by letting the theta value act on the log-shells rather than on the integral structure. The paper's (Ind3) is the upper semi-compatibility of the log-Kummer correspondence along the log-links (page 156).
  5. The finiteness assertion −|log(Θ)| ∈ ℝ of the corollary is built into the type (the value is real by construction).
  6. |log(q)| uses the normalization (1/2ℓ) log(q) from IUT IV; IUT III only says it "is easily computed in terms of the q-parameters".

Hypotheses of the top-level theorems. Iut.classicalABC_of_variant has exactly one hypothesis, h312 : Cor312VariantHolds. All other inputs (tower arithmetic, curve facts, Chebyshev bounds, existence of Θ-data for the Legendre curves) are discharged by closed terms in Iut/Tripod/Main.lean:120 to 132. h312 is not a restatement of abc: it is an inequality between log-volumes of local objects, and the passage to abc goes through IUT IV Theorem 1.10, Corollaries 2.2, 2.3 and [GenEll] Theorem 2.1. It carries, however, all of the content that IUT III is supposed to supply; the repository's own analysis (Iut/WallEquiv/README.md, Iut/WallEquiv/NonDerivable.lean:548, 556) proves that in its model of Theorem 3.11 the remaining step (Step (xi) of the proof of Corollary 3.12) is equivalent to an inequality it names after Scholze and Stix, that this step is not derivable from the fields of its Theorem 3.11 package, and that the step for all Θ-data implies ClassicalABC. Whether Cor312VariantHolds is true is not known; if it were false the main theorem would hold vacuously. Not checked by us: whether InitialThetaData.{0} is inhabited by a term that the definitions intend (the repository constructs Θ-data for Legendre curves in Iut/Concrete/Existence.lean).

2.4 How Theorem 3.11 is stated

Iut.LogLink.Thm311 L K : Prop (Iut/LogLink/Thm311.lean:141) is a structure with fields ind12, multiradial, upperSemi, logShell, volCompat, thetaCompat, about a LogThetaLattice D and "unit-induced" Kummer data LatticeKummer L of the repository's multi-container model, and thm311_model (line 190) proves it for every lattice and every such Kummer data. The Kummer identifications are scalings of the container (model_transport, line 170); the output of the multiradial algorithm is defined as lgp D S (model_out, line 167). This is a model in which the paper's Frobenioids, Hodge theaters and Kummer theory are replaced by local fields and a log-volume calculus; the repository says so. It is not IUT III, Theorem 3.11 as stated on pages 153 to 158, whose objects (D-prime-strips, Θ^{±ell}NF-Hodge theaters, the splitting monoids Ψ^⊥_LGP, global Frobenioids, the Kummer isomorphisms of [IUTchII] Corollaries 4.6 and 4.8) are not formalized there in the paper's sense. XiPrinciple (line 203) is Step (xi) as an explicit hypothesis; variant_of_thm311 (line 214) and xi_iff_ss (line 219) relate it to the variant and to SSIneq (Iut/Wall/MultiContainer.lean:88).

2.7 Reusable parts

Candidates to reuse after porting to Lean v4.36.0 and checking statements: the number theory in Iut/Tower/ (Serre's different bound Iut.Serre.not_pow_dvd_differentIdeal, ramification in towers), Iut/Torsion/ (division polynomials, E[n] ≅ (ℤ/n)² over algebraically closed fields of characteristic 0), Iut/Implication/ChebyshevExplicit.lean, Iut/NumberTheory/ (rational six exponentials theorem, linear independence of logarithms of primes), the formal-conjectures equivalences in Iut/Abc/FormalConjectures.lean, the tensor-packet log-volume construction in Iut/Concrete/LocalConstruct/, and the Θ-data record as one reading of IUT I, Definition 3.1 (to be compared with our own extraction). The Corollary 3.12 strand and the model of Theorem 3.11 are not reusable as statements of the papers; they can serve as a documented alternative interpretation.

5. google-deepmind/formal-conjectures

URL: https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures. Commit 9097e2f90e66d2f6244209cb1c07f7120d17ec53 (2026-11-24). Apache-2.0. Lean v4.33.1, Mathlib v4.33.1 (0df444a3). 2199 Lean files. A statement repository: 5510 sorry, 107 native_decide, one axiom (FormalConjecturesTest/Util/Linters/StubLinter.lean:39, a linter test). FormalConjecturesForMathlib/ has no sorry.

FormalConjectures/Wikipedia/ABC.lean (read in full) defines ABC.radical n = n.primeFactors.prod id, ABC.quality a b c = log c / log (radical (a*b*c)), and three open theorems: ABC.abc (finiteness, pairwise coprimality of {a, b, c}), ABC.abc.variants.lt_constant_mul (∃ K, no sign condition, strict <), ABC.abc.variants.quality (finiteness of quality > 1 + ε).

Comparison with Challenge.ABC: IUT/Applications/ABCFormalConjectures.lean restates the three as Prop definitions and proves abc_iff_ABC, ltConstantMul_iff_ABC, qualityVariant_iff_ABC (standard axioms only). All three are exactly equivalent to Challenge.ABC. Details: positivity of K is forced by the triple 1 + 1 = 2; strict and non-strict constant forms are equivalent by replacing K with K + 1; in the quality form the radical of abc is at least 2 on every triple with a, b > 0 and a + b = c (the radical is 1 only when abc = 1, i.e. a = b = c = 1, which violates a + b = c), so the division by log 1 = 0 never occurs and the quality set equals the finiteness set for each ε.

  1. S. Mochizuki, "On the formalization of IUT: a preliminary progress report [joint work in progress with Y. Hoshi, G. Yamashita, Y. Yang, ...]", slides, April 2026, 14 pages, https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Formalization%20of%20IUT%20(2026-04).pdf (server date 2026-04-07; SHA-256 9a32b248...72ee9c; talk at the online Workshop on AI and Theorem Provers in Mathematics, 8 to 10 April 2026, https://aitpm.github.io/). Read in full. Contents: Lean formalization viewed mainly as a communication tool; a five-stage plan (Stage 1: IUT III Theorem 3.11 implies Corollary 3.12; Stage 2: Theorem 3.11 modulo IUT I and II; Stages 3 to 5: earlier anabelian work and the numerics of IUT IV), stating that the project is "in the early skeletal portion of Stage 1"; a discussion of species and mutations and of formalizing ZFC as a first-order theory (judged not necessary for Stages 1 and 2); a review of IUT through a toy gluing of two copies of an integral domain along the N-th power map; and in §4 a description (a diagram, no code) of "skeletal Lean code for 3.11.5 ⇒ 3.12". There "3.11.5" is a reorganization "(= 3.11 + Rmk. 3.9.5)" that, as the slides say, never appears in IUT I to IV; the implication 3.11 ⇒ 3.12 is decomposed into four commuting triangles (a multiradial algorithm with input a basic prime-strip "BPS", a descent arrow (dsc), a "hull+det" step, and a "simultaneous holomorphic expressibility" arrow (SHE) for the q-pilot), with properties called input prime-strip link (IPL) and algorithmic parallel transport (APT). The skeletal code concerns the fourth triangle ("3.11.5 ⇒ 3.12"); code for the second and third is in progress. The slides state that the code is not yet suitable for release to the public. No Lean code is published; we found no repository.
  2. S. Mochizuki, "Report on the current situation surrounding inter-universal Teichmüller theory (IUT)", October 2025, 30 pages, https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/IUT-report-2025-10.pdf. Read: contents and §3.2 (pages 26 to 30). §3.2 describes Lean formalization activity at RIMS, states that efforts "have only just begun" and will take "at least several more years", and proposes reorganizing IUT into blackboxes plus purely formal arguments ((MthFm), (BBxFm)). No Lean code.

Found, opened only in part: Project LANA, "Interim report on IUT theory", dated 2026-07-16, 50 pages, https://ncatlab.org/nlab/files/LANAProject-Report-July2026.pdf. Only the abstract and table of contents were read: it treats the passage from Theorem 3.11 to Corollary 3.12, isolates a compatibility problem at the final stage, compares it with Scholze and Stix, and states that the project "has not yet reconstructed a proof of the required compatibility". This is the report cited as "[LANA]" in lana-agents/iut/Iut/WallEquiv/README.md.

Downloaded copies are kept in a local, untracked directory; the URLs above are the sources.

7. Mathlib

Our project uses Mathlib v4.36.0; the LANA projects use v4.32.0. Mathlib provides the radical (UniqueFactorizationMonoid.radical, Nat.radical_eq_prod_primeFactors, used in IUT/Applications/ABC.lean), number fields and their places, adic completions, PiTensorProduct, Haar measure, and Weierstrass curves. It has no anabelian geometry, Frobenioids, Hodge theaters, or tempered fundamental groups.
