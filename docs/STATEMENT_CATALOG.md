# Statement catalog

Generated by `scripts/gen_docs.py` from `data/statements.json`. Do not edit by hand.

Every numbered statement and every lettered result of the Introductions, grouped by paper and section, with the curated parts and proof steps under their parent. Titles are the parenthesized titles of the papers (for examples, the title phrase after the number), read from the PDF text layer only: subscripts, superscripts and some symbols of the printed titles are dropped or flattened. Statuses: NOT_EXTRACTED means that only the mechanical index has seen the statement. "Path" marks the critical path of `docs/CRITICAL_PATH.md`. PDF pages are the pages a reader gave in the curated record when there is one, listed span by span, with the paper in front of each span on another paper than the record's ("III 173 to 174; IV 23"), and otherwise the mechanical span from the header to the next recognized header.

Rows of kind "fragment" are Lean lemmas that check one piece of the parent above them; the Lean declaration column of a fragment names that lemma. A parent is formalized only when its own row names a declaration, which then states its entire content; its fragments never make it formalized or verified.

BLOCKED_BY_MISSING_JUSTIFICATION: the text gives an assertion where a formalization needs a derivation; this is not a claim that the result is false.
EXTERNAL_CLAIM: a statement of another paper, stated in Lean as a proposition and used only as a hypothesis; it is not proved in this project.
