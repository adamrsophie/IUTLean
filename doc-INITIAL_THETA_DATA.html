Initial Θ-data (IUT I, Definition 3.1)

Source: Mochizuki, Inter-universal Teichmüller Theory I, Definition 3.1, pages 61 to 63 (read as rendered images), Remarks 3.1.1 to 3.1.7, pages 63 to 69 (text). Existence: Inter-universal Teichmüller Theory IV, Corollary 2.2, pages 41 and 42 (rendered images), proof pages 43 to 46 (text, skimmed).

Lean: IUT/PaperI/InitialThetaData.lean, namespace IUT.PaperI. Curated records: data/curated/iut1.json, ids IUT1.Def3_1 and IUT1.Def3_1.a to IUT1.Def3_1.f.

The tuple

Initial Θ-data is a tuple (F̄/F, X_F, l, C_K, V, V^bad_mod, ε). Several further objects are derived from it inside the definition: G_F, E_F, C_F, F_mod, F_sol, V_mod, V^good_mod, K, X_K, V^bad, V^good, Π_v, and the arrow-decorated curves. The definition is stated condition by condition below. "Mathlib" means Lean 4 Mathlib at tag v4.36.0; paths are relative to Mathlib/.

(b) pages 61 and 62

Content, split into clauses:

  1. X_F is a once-punctured elliptic curve over F with stable reduction at every v ∈ 𝕍(F)^non; E_F is the elliptic curve it determines.
  2. C_F is the stack quotient of X_F by the natural action of ±1; F_mod is the field of moduli of X_F ([AbsTopIII] Definition 5.1 (ii)); F_sol is the maximal solvable extension of F_mod in F̄; V_mod = 𝕍(F_mod).
  3. V^bad_mod ⊆ V^non_mod is a nonempty set of valuations of odd residue characteristic such that X_F has bad (that is, multiplicative) reduction at the places over V^bad_mod; V^good_mod is the complement.
  4. Fundamental groups Π_{X_F} ⊆ Π_{C_F} and geometric parts Δ_X ⊆ Δ_C.
  5. F/F_mod is Galois of degree prime to l, and the 2 · 3-torsion points of E_F are defined over F.

Summary of what is in Lean

PartialInitialThetaConditions W l S (relative to k) bundles: (a); the torsion, reduction, bad-set, multiplicative and Galois clauses of (b); the prime and residue characteristic clauses of (c). Its docstring lists every missing clause. It is not initial Θ-data.

Statements of the paper that would sharpen the Lean: none was formalized. In particular the claim l ≠ 5 or any lower bound on l beyond 5 is not stated; it is not part of Definition 3.1.

Existence: IUT IV, Corollary 2.2

Pages 41 and 42 (rendered). Setting: X = P^1_Q, the λ-line, with divisor D = {0, 1, ∞}; a compactly bounded subset K_V whose support contains the prime 2 and satisfies the condition (∗_{j-inv}). Assertion (ii) gives constants H_unif, C_K, H_K, sets δ = 2^12 · 3^3 · 5 · d, a finite exceptional set Exc_d, and for an elliptic curve E over a number field with x_E ∈ K_V \ Exc_d it sets F_tpd = F_mod(E[2]) and F = F_mod(√−1, E_{F_mod}[2 · 3 · 5]). The conclusion is that E_F and F_mod arise from initial Θ-data as in IUT IV Theorem 1.10, satisfying conditions (C1) and (C2) there.

Proof outline as read on pages 43 to 46: a prime l is chosen with the prime number theorem (IUT IV Proposition 2.1) satisfying conditions (P1) to (P5); (P6), the SL_2 image, follows from (P2), (P4), (P5) and [GenEll] Lemma 3.1 (iii); (P7) states that conditions (a) to (f) follow formally; the existence of V and ε uses (P6).

Points a reader of the proof should check:

  • the condition that [F : F_mod] be prime to l (Definition 3.1 (b)) was not found argued separately on the pages read; it is covered only by the general statement (P7);
  • the F-rationality of the 2 · 3-torsion follows from F ⊇ F_mod(E[2 · 3 · 5]) as soon as E_F is the base change of a model of E over F_mod (an inference, not a quotation);
  • which of (P1) to (P5) give the residue characteristic and q-parameter conditions of (c) was not traced in detail.

What a formal nonvacuity proof would require

  1. Definitions of every object in (a) to (f): field of moduli, orbicurves and stacks, K-cores, étale and tempered fundamental groups, the Tate uniformization and q-parameters, cusps and the quotient Q of [EtTh].
  2. The structure theorem E[n](F̄) ≅ (Z/nZ)^2 and the mod l Galois representation.
  3. An open image result giving SL_2(F_l) ⊆ image for some prime l in the required range, which is the role of [GenEll] Lemma 3.1 (iii) and IUT IV Proposition 2.1.
  4. The prime number theorem in the form used by IUT IV Proposition 2.1. This is available: IUT/PaperIV/Proposition2_1.lean proves both parts of that proposition from Mathlib's Chebyshev.tendsto_theta_div_atTop, and the prime-selection statement is the theorem IUT.PaperIV.Proposition2_1.exists_prime_not_mem (for every finite set A there is a prime p ∉ A with p ≤ 2(θ_A + ξ_prm), for a fixed ξ_prm ≥ 5).
  5. The existence of an elliptic curve with the required bad reduction, for example from the λ-line setting of IUT IV.

A weaker, Lean-checkable target is nonvacuity of PartialInitialThetaConditions: no inhabitant is exhibited in Lean, so its consistency is an open obligation. A witness cannot have F = Q(i). TorsionRational W 6 requires every geometric 6-torsion point to be F-rational, hence the full 3-torsion; since the Weil pairing E[3] × E[3] → μ_3 is surjective and Galois equivariant, G_F then acts trivially on μ_3, so F contains a primitive cube root of unity. Q(i) does not, so no elliptic curve over Q(i) has full rational 6-torsion. Any witness field contains Q(√−1, √−3) = Q(ζ_12). (The Weil pairing is not in Mathlib, so this argument is not checked in Lean.)

One way a witness could be built, with no specific curve claimed to work:

  1. fix an elliptic curve E over a number field k (standing in for F_mod) and set F = k(E[6], √−1); then F contains √−1 and the 6-torsion is F-rational by construction;
  2. check that F/k is Galois (it is, as a compositum of Galois extensions) and compute its degree, which must be prime to the chosen l;
  3. check semistable reduction of E_F at every finite place of F, on minimal models, possibly after enlarging F, which changes step 2;
  4. choose a nonempty set S of places of k of odd residue characteristic over which E_F has multiplicative reduction, and check it at every place of F above S;
  5. choose a prime l ≥ 5 prime to [F : k] and to the residue characteristics in S.

Each step needs explicit computation (division polynomials, minimal models, reduction types) that has not been done, and Mathlib has no machinery yet to certify the degree [k(E[6]) : k].
