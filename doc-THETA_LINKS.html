Theta-link variants in IUT I to IUT III

This file compares the five theta-link variants and the induced D-link. Every cell cites the page it comes from. "Not stated" means the pages read say nothing on the point. "Not read" means the relevant pages were not read for this file.

Pages read for this file: IUT I pages 3, 33, 71, 82 to 86, 88, 89 (renders of 3, 71, 85, 88, 89 viewed; page 87 not read); IUT II pages 1 to 20, 36 to 39, 46 to 49, 55 to 58, 67 to 73, 76, 78 to 85, 93 to 96, 99 to 103, 105 to 108, 110 to 116, 120 to 173 (renders of 3, 16, 17, 47, 49, 72, 79, 80, 94, 122, 145, 158, 159, 160, 162 to 165 viewed); IUT III pages 109 to 114 (renders of 112, 113 viewed).

Notation in plain text: qq_v is the q with a double underline of IUT I, Example 3.2 (iv), page 71 (rendered): qq_v = q_v^{1/2l}, a chosen 2l-th root of the Tate parameter q_v of E_v, determined up to a μ_{2l}-multiple. The theta-link formulas below use qq_v, not q_v. HT is a Θ±ellNF-Hodge theater; † and ‡ mark the domain and codomain theaters. F^⊩ is an F^⊩-prime-strip; F^{⊩▶×μ} is an F^{⊩▶×μ}-prime-strip; F^{⊢×μ} is an F^{⊢×μ}-prime-strip; D^⊢ is a D^⊢-prime-strip.

A full poly-isomorphism A ⥲ B is the set of all isomorphisms A ⥲ B (IUT I section 0, page 33). It is a relation between A and B, not a single map. All five links below are full poly-isomorphisms; none is a function or a functor in the ordinary sense.

Points of doubt

  • IUT II Corollary 4.10 (page 160) cites the Θ-link of IUT I Corollary 3.7 (i); the IUT II Introduction refers to IUT I Theorem A (ii) for the same object (IUT II pages 15 to 17). The two descriptions were not compared in detail.
  • The LGP and lgp rows rely on IUT III pages 109 to 114 only. The log-link and the remarks after Remark 3.8.1 were not read, so most cells there say "not stated in the pages read".
  • "Invertible" for full poly-isomorphisms is a consequence of the IUT I page 33 definition, not a separate statement in Cor 3.7 or Cor 4.10.
