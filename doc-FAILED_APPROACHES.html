Failed and abandoned approaches

Approaches that were tried and dropped, or rejected before any code was written, with the reason. Only what the repository evidences is listed. Each entry says what statement was attempted or considered, under which assumptions, what the outcome was, and where the evidence is. A countermodel in docs/COUNTERMODELS.md refutes a simplified abstraction only; none refutes a statement of the papers.

Rejected because the result would be a shell

  • Prime strips, Hodge theaters, Θ-links (IUT I, IUT II). Interfaces with the paper's conclusions as fields could be written, but nothing would constrain them to be the paper's objects. Not formalized. Only the elementary first step is now stated, without conclusions as fields: poly-morphisms as sets of morphisms and capsules (IUT/PaperI/PolyMorphism.lean). Evidence: docs/PAPER_I_STRUCTURES.md, docs/PAPER_II_STRUCTURES.md.
  • **The log-shell as ℤ_p.** For odd p, replacing the log-shell of IUT III Definition 1.1 (page 24) by ℤ_p would typecheck but would not be the definition. Rejected. The faithful definition for the base field ℚ_p is now in IUT/Prerequisites/PadicLog.lean: the log-shell is (p*)⁻¹ · log(ℤ_p^×), with the p-adic logarithm proved convergent, a homomorphism and of image {z | ‖z‖ ≤ 1/p*}, and its equality with ℤ_p is a theorem (padicLogShell_eq_range_coe), not a definition. The identification of the Galois-invariant units with ℤ_p^× is an interpretation, not formalized. Finite extensions of ℚ_p remain open (OPEN_PROBLEMS.md, P-LOG).
  • **A Lean Prop for Theorem 3.11 or Corollary 3.12.** Rejected; see docs/DECISIONS.md, decision 4.
  • Adopting the inclusion of Step (xi-f) as a hypothesis. It restates the inequality conjunct of the conclusion (stepXIf_inclusion_iff_inequality); the finiteness conjunct of page 174 is separate. Rejected as a vacuity trick. Evidence: docs/COR312_ANALYSIS.md, condition C6.

Dropped because a statement was found to be vacuous

  • A lower bound on the blurring factor stated as a conclusion. Attempted statement: under (Lin), a cycle with the scalar j² (or its average l(l+1)/12) on one edge that closes up to a factor B forces B ≥ j² (or B ≥ l(l+1)/12). Assumption: the tolerance model, which already says that the product of the scalars is at most B. Outcome: the conclusion was the hypothesis restated after evaluating the product. Both forms were withdrawn; what remains is the evaluation itself, stated as an equivalence (blur_bound, consistentUpTo_avgSq_iff in IUT/Audit/ScholzeStix/Blurring.lean), with the averaging identity avgSq_eq as the only content beyond the hypothesis.

Blocked on definitions or literature

  • **IUT I Definition 3.1, conditions (d), (e), (f) and the SL_2 condition.** They need the field K, the q-parameter and the field of moduli of the curve, which are not available in the needed form. Only a partial collection is stated (PartialInitialThetaConditions). Evidence: docs/INITIAL_THETA_DATA.md.
  • Pull-back invariance of the degree of arithmetic divisors (IUT IV Definition 1.9 (i)). The paper defines the pull-back "in the evident fashion"; choosing a pull-back so that the identity holds would make it nearly definitional. Deferred until the convention of [GenEll] §1 is fixed. Evidence: docs/MATHLIB_GAP_ANALYSIS.md.
  • Degree zero of principal arithmetic divisors. With weight 1 at every archimedean place, a coefficient convention at complex places would have to be invented. Not defined; only the product formula was formalized. Evidence: docs/DECISIONS.md, decision 7.
  • **A Grothendieck universe in ZFSet.** Mathlib provides the embedding at the PSet level only. The universe results take the existence axiom as the hypothesis ExistenceAxiom, which is independent of what Lean proves about ZFSet.{u}; the conditional results are vacuous in models where it fails.
  • Morphisms of IUT I Definition 6.13. Not found on the pages read. Evidence: docs/PAPER_I_STRUCTURES.md.

A reading that conflicts with the text

  • IUT II Remark 2.6.3 (d), page 80. Read literally, ‖Γ′‖ is strictly increasing in |Γ′| on {1, …, l*}, and Lean proves this (gammaNormValue_strictMono). The remark says "not strictly increasing" and "constant for |Γ′| ≥ l*", which the literal reading makes false and vacuous respectively. The intended reading is not settled. Evidence: docs/PAPER_II_STRUCTURES.md.

Not attempted

The following were planned but have no code: the nonvacuity of PartialInitialThetaConditions (an explicit curve), power maps on ℤ_p^×, and free and surface groups for IUT I §§1 to 2. The passage from IUT IV Theorem A to the abc statement now exists at the level of statements: IUT/Applications/ABCSpecialization.lean proves that a statement about rational points of ℙ¹ \ {0, 1, ∞} is equivalent to Challenge.ABC (vojta_iff_ABC). The remaining gap is named there: IUT IV proves the compactly bounded form (ii) of [GenEll] Theorem 2.1, and reaching that statement needs the direction (ii) ⟹ (i), whose proof uses points of bounded degree over number fields and a noncritical Belyi map; neither is formalized (OPEN_PROBLEMS.md, T-GENELL).
