Contributing

external claim (EXTERNAL_CLAIM): a statement of another paper, stated in Lean as a proposition and used only as a hypothesis; it is not proved in this project.

Contributions are welcome: a Lean proof of an open step, a faithful statement of a result that is not yet stated, a correction to the catalog, or a countermodel to a simplified abstraction. Read OPEN_PROBLEMS.md and docs/COR312_ANALYSIS.md first. No single step settles the abc conjecture or Corollary 3.12.

Trust policy

Everything in IUT/ outside IUT/Experimental/, and everything in Solution.lean and Challenge/, follows these rules. CI rejects a change that breaks them.

  • Axioms. Only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. No declaration may depend on any other axiom, and in particular on sorryAx.
  • Forbidden constructs. sorry, admit, axiom, opaque, unsafe, partial, native_decide, @[implemented_by], @[extern], debug.* options, set_option with an option outside the allowed list of the source scan, new syntax, macros, elaborators or notation, run_cmd and the other commands that run code at elaboration time, #eval, instances (commands, attributes, deriving) other than the one allowlisted line, default_instance attributes, native evaluation (decide +native, Lean.ofReduceBool, Lean.trustCompiler), export, open ... renaming, open ... hiding, and an open of a namespace outside the list ALLOWED_OPENS of the scan. The full list is in the header of scripts/Controls/source_scan.py. Challenge.lean alone may contain sorry, on purpose.
  • Names. No declaration may capture a name of a dependency or of Challenge.* by name resolution: no IUT.X.Real.log, no IUT.X.Challenge.ABC, and no name declared directly in a namespace whose last component is a constant of an opened namespace (IUT.X.log against Real.log). Check 6 of AxiomGate.lean gives the exact rule and its allowlist; prefer a specific name to an entry there.
  • No placeholders and no shells. A Lean Prop for a result of the papers exists only when every term in it is genuinely defined. A structure whose fields are the conclusions of the paper, a hypothesis that restates the conclusion, or a definition chosen so that the statement becomes trivial or vacuous is not allowed. When a statement cannot be written faithfully, record it as an obligation in the catalog instead.
  • Partial collections are named as such. A structure that captures only some of the conditions of a definition carries Partial in its name, and its docstring lists the conditions it omits (see PartialInitialThetaConditions).
  • The experimental directory. Drafts with sorry go in IUT/Experimental/. No module outside it may import it, IUT.lean never lists it, and nothing in it counts as verified. See IUT/Experimental/README.md.
  • Trusted definitions and the challenge baseline. Challenge/Defs.lean imports Mathlib only. A change to it is reviewed by hand against its source and then recorded with python3 -I scripts/Controls/fingerprints.py --write. The seven statements of Challenge.lean and the list of names in comparator.json form the challenge baseline: a change to any of them (a statement, a constant, a hypothesis, a name) needs a separate approval, by a reviewer other than the author of the change, after a comparison with the cited source. A matching change in Solution.lean passes the comparator, so the comparator alone does not protect the baseline. No mechanism enforces the approval: fingerprints.py --write records whatever is current, and .github/CODEOWNERS names a single owner, so a change authored by that owner cannot receive a code-owner approval from someone else. The rule binds by agreement until a second owner is named.

Every Lean module cites its source

Each module starts with a docstring that gives:

  • the paper, the numbered statement, and the manuscript pages, written "pages 173 to 186";
  • which pages were read, and whether the formulas were checked against rendered pages or only against the extracted text (which loses decorations such as ≠, ∉, ceilings and floors);
  • every interpretation choice, with the reason for it;
  • the reviewed scope of each declaration: which statement, part or proof step of the papers it states in full, or, for a lemma that checks only a piece, which display or sentence it covers and what it leaves out.

A pull request that adds or changes a Lean statement tied to the papers must cite the source page for it; a statement without a page is not accepted.

A countermodel module also says which simplified abstraction it refutes, and that it does not refute a statement of the papers unless that is shown.

How statements enter the catalog

data/statements.json is the index of every numbered statement of the four papers. It is generated: do not edit it by hand. Records written by reading the papers live in data/curated/iut1.json to iut4.json, one record per statement id (for example IUT3.Cor3_12.StepXI.f). The fields are source, content, explicit_hypotheses, implicit_hypotheses, conclusion, definitions_required, results_used, external_dependencies, lean_declaration, statement_status, proof_status, obligations and ambiguities.

A record may name a lean_declaration and carry a formalized or verified status only if the declaration states the entire content of the record. A Lean lemma that checks one arithmetic or elementary step gets its own fragment record <parent id>.frag.<short name> with "kind": "fragment", parent, covers and not_covered, and the parent lists it in fragments (docs/DECISIONS.md, decision 12).

Statement fidelity and proof status are reported separately. The status values are:

StatusMeaning
NOT_EXTRACTEDlocated by the mechanical index; content not read
EXTRACTEDread and recorded in the curated data
INTERPRETATION_AMBIGUOUSread; more than one reading is possible, and the record lists them
LEAN_STATEMENT_DRAFTa Lean statement exists but captures only part of the statement, or is not yet reviewed as faithful
STATEMENT_FORMALIZEDa faithful Lean statement of the whole record exists in the library
STATEMENT_REVIEWEDthe Lean statement was reviewed against the rendered pages
PROOF_IN_PROGRESSa proof is being written
BLOCKED_BY_MATHLIBneeds a definition or theorem that Mathlib lacks
BLOCKED_BY_EXTERNAL_THEOREMneeds a result of another paper that is not formalized
BLOCKED_BY_MISSING_JUSTIFICATIONthe text gives an assertion where a formalization needs a derivation; this is not a claim that the result is false
CONDITIONALLY_VERIFIEDproved in Lean under hypotheses stated in the record
KERNEL_VERIFIEDproved in Lean, with no hypothesis beyond those of the statement, from the allowed axioms only
INDEPENDENTLY_CHECKEDkernel verified and also checked independently
COUNTERMODEL_FOUNDa Lean countermodel to the faithfully stated statement

A record that claims a verified status must name a lean_declaration that exists in the built library and depends only on the allowed axioms; scripts/dashboard.py does not count it as verified otherwise, and a record of the proved family (conditional ones included) whose declaration depends on another axiom is a validation error. A fragment id is <parent>.frag.<name> with exactly the parent field as prefix. Page fields (source.pages or source.pdf_pages) are read by scripts/records.py: "page 7", "pages 41 to 43", "pages 10 to 12, 15", and, for spans on several papers, "IUT III pages 173 to 174; IUT IV page 23"; any other form is a validation error.

A fragment whose declaration tests a model or an example (a simplified setting, an interval model, a countermodel to an abstraction) rather than checking a step of the papers has "scope": "model_test". The dashboard counts such fragments on their own line, "model tests", and never among the fragments checked in Lean. model_test is the one fixed value of scope; any other value is free text describing the scope, and model_test is allowed only on fragment records.

Statements of other papers (the explicit estimates paper, Zhou's papers, Dupuy and Hilado) go in data/curated/external.json, with an id prefix registered in EXTERNAL_PAPERS of scripts/records.py (EXPL.Thm5_4, ZHOU1.Thm3_2). A parent has "kind": "external_statement", no parent, and in source the fields authors, title, arxiv (an identifier listed for the prefix) or journal, statement (its number in that paper) and pages_in_paper ("pages 27 to 29"); never pages or pdf_pages, which are pages of IUT I to IV. Its lean_declaration is null unless a def ... : Prop states it exactly; then its statuses are STATEMENT_FORMALIZED and EXTERNAL_CLAIM (stated, assumed, not proved), and the definition is registered in authorStatements of AxiomGate.lean, so that no declaration proves it outright. Its fragments follow the fragment rules above; a conditional fragment lists the claims it assumes in obligations, which the dashboard prints. A record of IUT I to IV may also give pages of another paper it relies on in source.pages_in_paper. The definitions of the dashboard counts are in the docstring of scripts/dashboard.py. After editing curated data, regenerate the derived files:

sh scripts/run_all.sh

This runs every stage that needs no PDF and fails on an invalid record, a stale build, or a missing Graphviz. The stage that reads the four manuscripts is separate (sh scripts/run_all.sh --pdfs <pdf-dir>). See scripts/README.md.

Classifying a result

Every gap or failure is put in one of seven categories, and the categories are not mixed:

  1. failure of proof search;
  2. missing library theorem;
  3. gap in the formalization;
  4. insufficient hypotheses;
  5. counterexample to a simplified abstraction;
  6. counterexample to a faithfully formalized statement;
  7. demonstrated contradiction.

A hypothesis the paper leaves unstated but that holds is not an error in the paper. A countermodel to a simplified model is category 5, never 6 or 7.

Running every check locally

From the repository root, after lake exe cache get:

python3 -I scripts/Controls/source_scan.py
python3 -I scripts/gen_root.py --check
lake build
lake build Challenge
python3 -I scripts/Controls/fingerprints.py --check
git ls-files -- IUT.lean IUT > tracked-files.txt
AXIOM_GATE_TRACKED_FILES=tracked-files.txt lake env lean AxiomGate.lean
AXIOM_GATE_TRACKED_FILES=tracked-files.txt sh GateControls/run.sh AxiomGate.lean

lake comparator and lake check need the bubblewrap sandbox and therefore Linux; CI runs them on every push and pull request. A new library file must be imported by IUT.lean: run python3 -I scripts/gen_root.py to regenerate it.

Writing rules for documents

  • Self-contained wording: a document describes the project and the papers, not the process that produced it.
  • Page ranges are written "pages 173 to 186". Cite the manuscript page, not the journal page.
  • American English spelling.
  • No dash used as a sentence connector or parenthetical; use a semicolon, a colon or a period.
  • Do not overstate. Say what is proved, under which hypotheses, and what is not; "the paper does not argue X" is not "X is false".
  • Do not hand-edit generated files (docs/DASHBOARD.md, docs/CRITICAL_PATH.md, docs/DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md, docs/STATEMENT_CATALOG.md, docs/SOURCE_MANIFEST.md, docs/REFERENCE_INVENTORY.md, docs/dependency_graph.svg, data/statements.json).
  • Do not quote counts from the dashboard in hand-written documents; link to it.

What happens to a pull request

CI runs the checks above. A reviewer reads the Lean statement against the cited pages, checks the docstring, and checks that the catalog records were updated. A change to Challenge/Defs.lean, to a statement of Challenge.lean, or to comparator.json needs a separate review against its source and a separate approval.
