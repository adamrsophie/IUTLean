Design decisions

Each decision gives what was chosen and why. A decision changes only by a new entry that says which earlier one it replaces.

1. Toolchain and Mathlib pin

Lean 4.36.0 (lean-toolchain: leanprover/lean4:v4.36.0) with Mathlib at tag v4.36.0, commit fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842 (lakefile.toml).

Rationale: a tagged Mathlib release matched to the toolchain gives a reproducible build and a prebuilt cache (lake exe cache get). Every Mathlib name cited in docs/MATHLIB_GAP_ANALYSIS.md was checked against this version, so a bump requires rechecking those claims.

2. Material kept out of the repository

The PDFs of the four papers, the secondary literature (the manuscript of Scholze and Stix, Mochizuki's reports and comments), clones of other Lean projects, and working notes are kept in a local, untracked directory. The PDFs are not redistributed. data/source_manifest.json records the file name, SHA-256, title page date and page count of each PDF in hand, so that a reader with a copy can confirm it is the same version.

Rationale: the papers are copyrighted, and the project's claims must be checkable from the tracked files plus a copy of the papers. The pipeline scripts/run_all.sh takes the PDF directory as an argument.

3. Trusted definitions, challenge and solution

  • Challenge/Defs.lean holds the only trusted vocabulary: rad, ABC, ABCFinite, ABCLog. It imports Mathlib only, is reviewed by hand, and is fingerprinted in data/challenge_fingerprints.json.
  • Challenge.lean states the headline theorems with sorry; Solution.lean states them under the same names and proves them from the library. lake comparator checks that the two statements agree and that the proofs use only the allowed axioms (comparator.json).
  • Challenge is one library, not two, because Lake assigns modules to libraries by root prefix; it is not a default target, so lake build and lake check never compile the file with sorry.

Rationale: a reader who trusts Mathlib and reads one short file can judge what the headline theorems mean, without reading the library.

4. No Lean Prop for Theorem 3.11 or Corollary 3.12

Neither result is stated in Lean. Their statements mention Hodge theaters, the log-theta-lattice, tensor packets of log-shells, the indeterminacies (Ind1) to (Ind3) and the holomorphic hull of possible images, none of which is defined in the library.

Rationale: a statement with undefined terms replaced by arbitrary types or by structure fields would be a shell: it could be proved, or assumed, without saying anything about the papers. The project records the missing vocabulary as obligations instead (OPEN_PROBLEMS.md).

5. "Partial" in the names of partial condition collections

A structure that captures only some of the conditions of a definition carries Partial in its name, and its docstring lists what it omits. Example: PartialInitialThetaConditions in IUT/PaperI/InitialThetaData.lean states conditions (a), parts of (b) and (c) of IUT I Definition 3.1, relative to an arbitrary base field, and omits (d), (e), (f) and the SL_2 condition.

Rationale: a reader who sees InitialThetaData would assume the full definition. The name must not claim more than the structure states.

6. The numerical layer of Corollary 3.12

IUT/PaperIII/Corollary312/Numerical.lean takes the two numbers −|log(Θ)| ∈ ℝ ∪ {+∞} and −|log(q)| ∈ ℝ as given (PilotLogVolumes) and proves what follows from their shape alone:

  • the constant form of the conclusion and its equivalence with the inequality when −|log(Θ)| is finite (conclusion_iff_constantForm, constantForm_of_top);
  • the reduction at the start of the proof (inequality_of_nonneg);
  • that the inclusion −|log(q)| ∈ R_{≤−|log(Θ)|} of Step (xi-f) is the conclusion itself (stepXIf_inclusion_iff_inequality, by Iff.rfl);
  • how Theorem 1.10 of IUT IV uses the conclusion (thm1_10_final_of_cor312).

What it shows: the last step of the proof of Corollary 3.12 is formal, so all the content of the corollary lies in establishing the inclusion, which needs the constructions listed in decision 4. The structure has no field that assumes a conclusion of the paper. The file is not a formalization of Corollary 3.12, and its docstring says so.

7. Archimedean weights

IUT/PaperIV/ArithmeticDivisor.lean gives every archimedean place weight 1 in the degree map, real or complex, exactly as in the display of IUT IV Definition 1.9 (i). IUT/Prerequisites/ProductFormula.lean uses Mathlib's convention, with multiplicity 2 at a complex place (NumberField.InfinitePlace.mult), for which the product formula holds.

Rationale: each module follows its source exactly. IUT IV pages 21 to 23 do not fix the coefficient of a principal divisor at a complex place; [GenEll] page 4 does, with the factor [F_v : ℝ] in the coefficient, and with that convention the degree of Definition 1.9 (i) vanishes on principal divisors (degPb_degF_principal in IUT/PaperIV/DegreePullback.lean).

8. Mechanical citation edges over-approximate dependence

data/dependencies.json contains an edge whenever a statement's text or proof cites another statement. docs/CRITICAL_PATH.md is everything reachable from IUT IV Theorem A and Corollary 2.3 along these edges and the curated ones.

Rationale: a citation is not a logical dependence, but missing a real dependence is worse than including a spurious one. The critical path is a reading list, not a bound in either direction: it includes statements cited only for context, and it misses dependences that the text uses without a citation or that the citation parser could not resolve. The documents say so.

9. Status vocabulary

Statement fidelity and proof status are two separate fields with fixed values (listed in CONTRIBUTING.md). scripts/extract_statements.py reports any other value, and scripts/dashboard.py counts a record as verified only if its Lean declaration exists in the built library and depends only on the allowed axioms.

Rationale: progress is measured by statements, not by lines of Lean. A formalized statement that is not proved, and a proof under extra hypotheses, must not be counted as a verified result.

10. Seven categories of result

Every gap is placed in one of seven categories, from failure of proof search to demonstrated contradiction (OPEN_PROBLEMS.md). Countermodels in IUT/Countermodels/ are always in the category "counterexample to a simplified abstraction" unless a docstring proves otherwise.

Rationale: the dispute about the theory makes overstatement costly. A model in which a simplified step fails says that a hypothesis is needed, not that the paper is wrong.

11. License

GNU General Public License, version 3 (LICENSE), for the Lean sources, the scripts and the documents.

Rationale: the same license as the sibling projects of the same author, and one that keeps derived formalizations open.

12. Fragment records (amends decision 9)

A Lean lemma that checks one arithmetic or elementary step of a statement of the papers does not give the whole statement a formalized or verified status. A record for a statement (or a part or proof step of one) may carry a lean_declaration and a status among STATEMENT_FORMALIZED, STATEMENT_REVIEWED, CONDITIONALLY_VERIFIED, KERNEL_VERIFIED, INDEPENDENTLY_CHECKED only if that declaration states the entire content of the record, with the same objects, hypotheses and conclusion. Otherwise its lean_declaration is null and its status says what is open. Each lemma that checks a piece gets its own record <parent id>.frag.<short name> with "kind": "fragment", its parent, exactly the hypotheses and conclusion of the Lean lemma, what of the parent it covers and what it does not_covered; the parent lists its fragments. A fragment is KERNEL_VERIFIED when the lemma has no hypothesis standing for an unproved claim of the papers, and otherwise CONDITIONALLY_VERIFIED with the assumed claims in obligations. The dashboard counts full statements and fragments separately.

Rationale: decision 9 checked that a linked declaration exists and uses only the allowed axioms, but not that it states the cataloged result. A kernel-checked fragment then made a whole statement of the papers look formalized or verified. Separate records keep the evidence and its scope together.

13. Working instructions for automated assistants stay out of the repository

Working instructions for automated assistants are not tracked. What a contributor needs is in tracked documents: the trust policy and writing rules in CONTRIBUTING.md, the decisions in this file, the trust boundary in docs/VERIFICATION_REPORT.md, the open obligations in OPEN_PROBLEMS.md, and the plan in docs/ROADMAP.md.

Rationale: the repository must be checkable and continuable from a clean checkout by a person who has only the tracked files and a copy of the papers. Every rule that matters for the mathematics or for trust is therefore stated in a tracked document, and nothing in the repository depends on untracked instructions.
